# Arm A blind listening check: results (2026-10-16)

Run under the [plan](arm-a-listening-plan.md).
- **One listener (the user):** 8 trials and one catch trial (A = B).
- **What was compared:** each trial plays the record's held half, then arm A's current best (a
  52-render rebuilt-DI search shrunk halfway) and the starting preset S, in balanced random letter
  order.
- **Answers:** `1S 2S 3S 4B 5B 6A 7A 9S`. Trial 8 was unanswered: "A and B sound distorted with
  noise".

| Trial | Part, amp | Judge (log, arm A vs S) | Heard |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | First Offering GTR 1, SW50R | +0.047 (S) | same; the record leaks |
| 2 | Long Way Home, PR12 | −0.049 (A) | same |
| 3 | Bloomlight, PR12 | +0.128 (S) | same |
| 4 | It Was My Fault For Waiting 2, AC20 | −0.103 (A) | **arm A** |
| 5 | femme, SW50R | −0.153 (A) | **arm A** |
| 6 | Today's The Day, AC20 | −0.229 (A) | **arm A** |
| 7 | Drag Me Down, SW50R | +0.052 (S) | **arm A** |
| 8 | Prodigal, PR12 | −0.111 (A) | no answer; both versions distorted and noisy |
| 9 (catch) | colour me red, PR12 | identical | same (correct) |

- **Where a difference was heard, arm A was chosen every time:** 4 of 4, exact 90% interval
  [0.47, 1.00]. That would happen about 1 time in 16 by chance.
- **Counting "same" as not arm A:** 4 of 7, [0.23, 0.87].
- **Agreement with the judge's sign:** 3 of 4. On trial 7 the judge slightly preferred S and the
  ear preferred arm A.
- **The differences heard were the larger ones:** the judge's |log| was 0.10–0.23, plus trial 7 at
  0.05. Trials 1–3 were at 0.05–0.13, and 1 leaked.
- **The catch trial was answered correctly.**

**Reading (descriptive, one listener):**
- Arm A's changes are audible on about half the songs, and where they are audible they sound closer
  to the record.
- The smaller changes weren't heard.
- This is the first listening support for song-only generated settings.

**Data-quality notes passed on:**
- Trial 1's record leaks other instruments.
- Trial 8's renders sound distorted and noisy, on both versions.

Both are checked by the bleed measurement under way, and trial 8's DI and renders will be looked at.
