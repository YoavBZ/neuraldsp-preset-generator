# End-to-end runs of `generate` on real songs (2026-10-13)

Each run was a fresh, sandboxed `claude -p` session with the plugin as a user gets it
(0.7.108 for the first two, 0.7.109 after #183 for the rest), a user-style prompt, the
song file, and no listening page. Presets were not installed. One listener, by ear, not
a declared test.

| song, part | pack | research | `generate`'s class | heard closer by ear |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hotel California, dual lead 4:20–5:00 | Morgan | documented: Les Paul into a cranked Tweed Deluxe, no pedals | crunch (PR12), clean start beside it | the crunch preset |
| Long Road Out of Eden, "the clean electric", no time given | Tone King | thin | chose a window from the mix itself (1:32–1:52) | not listened (the gap #183 fixed) |
| Long Road Out of Eden, electric solo 5:25–6:10 | Tone King | nothing documents the rig | crunch guessed (Squishy Lead), clean start beside it | the clean start (Vintage Trem) |
| the same solo | Morgan | the same | crunch guessed (SW50R Hair Metal), clean start beside it | the clean start (Pedal Platform Clean) |

- **How it was heard:** the user-supplied isolated part (Hotel California: the original
  minus its backing track; Eden: a separated lead stem) was the reference, and both presets
  were played through a DI the ML session's C′ network rebuilt from that part, on a blind
  A/B page. Both rebuilds sounded dirtier than the record (a known limit of the rebuilt DI).
- **What it showed:** with a documented rig, the main preset was right; with nothing
  documented, the guessed "driven" was wrong on both packs and the second start caught it.
- **What it changed:** the first two runs led to #183 (no default effects, don't guess the
  part's window, research the part not the song, disclose pedal stand-ins).
- Notes are in the user's learned notes (`packs/morgan/learned-tones.md`,
  `packs/toneking/learned-tones.md` under the data folder).

## 2026-10-22: 0.7.117 on the Hotel California solo

The same prompt and song, the plugin as shipped at 0.7.117 (sandboxed `claude -p`, no
renders). Two presets as before: the crunch start (PR12, its pedals standing in for a cranked
Tweed Deluxe, sourced) and a clean start pushed by its volume (49% to 80%, a stated guess).
The new arrangement prior was written and passed `arrangement.py check`, with sources and
confidence per item, and the 260-300 s window recorded with `time_basis: "user"`. But it
covered only the solo (Felder and Walsh, two sections) and left the rhythm guitars out ("not
researched; out of scope for the solo"); the skill now asks for the whole song, not only the part asked for (0.7.118).
The fully-clean volume rule was not exercised: the part is documented as crunch.
