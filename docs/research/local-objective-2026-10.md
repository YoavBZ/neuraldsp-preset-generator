# Split the local failure before fixing it

This report builds on [di-recovery-2026-10.md](di-recovery-2026-10.md), [di-rebuild-fine-structure-2026-10.md](di-rebuild-fine-structure-2026-10.md) and [song-to-settings-rethink-2026-10.md](song-to-settings-rethink-2026-10.md), and does not repeat them.

**Bottom line.** Before anyone builds a better objective near S, the project should find out which of four simultaneous changes inverts it. A better rebuild network is not the answer. The committed facts are these. Arm A, a knob search through C′'s rebuilt DI, reaches **−0.078 against S**, about a third of the true-DI search's −0.269. All of that gain comes in the first 52 renders. Stopping there and shrinking halfway to S keeps it: **−0.083 [−0.135, −0.032], 12 of 14 bands**. Near S, though, C′'s objective ranks settings at **Spearman −0.126 [−0.54, +0.29] against the true objective**, and the other rebuilds do no better on average (1z −0.127, 1a −0.121, 1b −0.098, their ensemble −0.113), while the menu is ranked at about 0.78 ([levers results](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/d24412a/docs/arm-a-levers-results.md?plain=1#L30-L69); [audit, a7b](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/e623c41/docs/audits/arm-a-2026-10-16/levers-audit/a7b_local.out?plain=1#L1-L6)) [V]. C′ is −0.35 on the Prodigal part's band, −0.17 on just-don't-talk's and +0.14 on Eggy's three parts. The nets differ by band, not on average: 1a and 1b move Eggy up by 0.16–0.19 and just-don't-talk down by 0.13–0.15 [V]. The local comparison that produced −0.126 changes four things at once: the DI, the reference (separated stem against the isolated `reference.wav`), the lag (−52 against the part's `judge_lag`) and the activity mask. Nothing committed separates them [V]. Two of the four, the DI and the stem, stay in any song-only product [I]. The best-supported mechanism is level. Both DIs are set to −22.9 LUFS, but C′'s peaks sit 3–5 dB low and its quiet passages 15–20 dB too loud. Input gain sits in front of the drive, so a DI level error acts as an input-gain error whose sign depends on the part's mix of attacks and sustain. The committed per-knob split supports half of it. Along the true-DI search paths, the true objective gets worse as input gain rises (mean ρ +0.17) while C′'s gets slightly better (−0.09), and where the true trend is clear C′'s sign matches on only 3 of 13 ([a7c](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/e623c41/docs/audits/arm-a-2026-10-16/levers-audit/a7c_dims.out?plain=1#L4)) [V]. So C′ wants more drive than the record does, as squashed peaks predict. But the tone knobs are inverted too: the sw50r's Mid, Level and Bass agree on 0 of 10 clear cases, and all knobs together on 42 of 101 ([a7c](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/e623c41/docs/audits/arm-a-2026-10-16/levers-audit/a7c_dims.out?plain=1#L1-L17)) [V]. So level is not the whole bias, and the mechanism itself is untested [I]. The plan below is ranked and costed. Step 0 costs 2–4 renders plus zero-render reads of stored data. Experiment 1 builds a 14-band local set with saved FLAC audio and a four-way decomposition (about 7k renders, about 1.4 hours at about 5k renders per hour). Experiment 2 tests level and drive calibration and input-gain profiling (about 7–15k). Experiment 3 tests hybrid pipelines (0.9–8.5k). Altogether that is 15–34k renders and about 3–7 hours of rendering. The running total is about 147–149k of the 200k limit, so the plan alone would take it to about 162–183k. Arm B's approved sound-space plan adds about 18k if it runs first, which would make it about 180–201k [I]. Until something beats it, "52 renders, then shrink halfway" stays the product rule. The DAC codec route passed its two-point opening rule (margin 0.168 against the true DI's 0.188), but it has never been tested locally. It is a 10–25% long shot, worth a 600-render ceiling test and no training yet.

## Four confounds and one level error can each invert the local order

### Why coarse ranking survives a bias that wrecks fine ranking

Menu presets differ by whole amp, cab and drive profiles. Their true-d differences are large, so a smooth bias that depends on the setting changes few of their pairwise orders. Near S, the K_S path points differ by small steps, from 1/8 down to 1/64 of a knob's range ([`knob_ceiling.py` L135–L177](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/d24412a/learn/knob_ceiling.py?plain=1#L135-L177)) [V]. There the same bias can outweigh the true differences, and the Spearman falls to zero or below [I]. In pairwise terms, mapped through the bivariate-normal relation, Spearman −0.126 is a pairwise accuracy of about **0.46**, and the menu's 0.776 is about **0.79** [I]. The surrogate-assisted optimisation literature gives a scale for these numbers. In a controlled study, a surrogate used for pre-selection helped only from a pairwise accuracy of about 0.6 and kept improving as accuracy rose, while the other two ways of using it (individual-based and generation-based) levelled off from about 0.7–0.8 ([Hanawa et al. 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.00844v1)) [V]. The authors note that their injected errors were uniform, without the local biases real models have [V]. A local Spearman of about 0.3 (pairwise accuracy about 0.60) is therefore roughly where a fine-move objective starts to pay [I]. The ranking-error measure itself, the fraction of violated pairs on new points, is the one s*ACM-ES uses to decide how long to trust its surrogate ([Loshchilov et al. 2012](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1204.2356)) [V].

### The four differences, and which of them a product can change

The rebuilt objective is `rebuilt_distance(stem, render, rebuilt, lag=−52)` ([`song_settings.py` L134–L140](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/d24412a/learn/song_settings.py?plain=1#L134-L140)). The true objective is the judge through the measure DI against `reference.wav` at the part's `judge_lag`. The activity mask is computed from whichever DI is passed in ([`aligned.py` L429–L432](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/d24412a/analysis/aligned.py?plain=1#L429-L432); [`rebuilt_judge.py` L34–L46](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/d24412a/learn/rebuilt_judge.py?plain=1#L34-L46)) [V].

Three readings follow [I]. The mask is probably null here as well, but only half of that is checked. The measure DIs have no frame 40 dB below their peak, so they mark nearly every frame as playing [V]. Nobody has checked the rebuilt side, and when the rebuilt DI's mask makes the judge refuse, `rebuilt_distance` already falls back to the stem as the mask ([`rebuilt_judge.py` L44–L45](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/d24412a/learn/rebuilt_judge.py?plain=1#L44-L45)) [V]. How often that fallback fired per net is not committed (below). The lag deserves a test rather than dismissal. Its shift of at most 0.003–0.03 in margins is the same order as plausible true-d differences between neighbouring settings, and it only matters if it varies with the setting. A constant shift leaves ranks unchanged. The reference is the confound that matters most for the product. If swapping the stem for `reference.wav` restores most of the local ranking, then no DI fix can work on songs, and the effort belongs in separation or in judge-side masks. The "same for every net" result points the same way. 1a and 1b cut gap fill by about 11 dB and closed much of C′'s treble deficit ([amendment 1](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/d24412a/docs/di-fine-structure-plan.md?plain=1#L567-L595); [screen](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/e623c41/docs/audits/arm-a-2026-10-16/exp4-screen-f3.json?plain=1#L14-L24)) [V]. On fold 3's known-target control, they closed 1 of 4 cells (mean shortfall +0.164 and +0.197), against C′'s 2 of 4 (+0.201) and the true DI's 4 of 4 (−0.043), and the fold 0–2 fine-tunes were stopped ([step 2](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/e623c41/docs/audits/arm-a-2026-10-16/exp4-step2.json?plain=1#L8-L89); [ledger](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/e623c41/docs/dev-test-ledger.json?plain=1#L376)) [V]. Their mean local fidelity did not move either (−0.121 and −0.098 against −0.126). Their band means did move, in opposite directions: Eggy up by 0.16–0.19, Timo And The Timezone down by 0.13–0.15 ([a7b](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/e623c41/docs/audits/arm-a-2026-10-16/levers-audit/a7b_local.out?plain=1#L2-L6)) [V]. On 3 bands, that can't be told from noise [I]. Whatever drives the bias is either something all rebuilds share (the stem, the integrated-loudness rule, the reconstruction of the amp-compressed envelope, the lag) or something the nets did not fix (sub-10 ms peak structure) [I].

### Level is the leading mechanism, and it acts through input gain

Both DIs are normalised to the same integrated loudness, −22.9 LUFS (BS.1770, via pyloudnorm) ([`direc_check.py` L38–L43](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/d24412a/learn/direc_check.py?plain=1#L38-L43)) [V]. At equal loudness, the control cells' RMS levels match within 0.7 dB. Crest factor, however, drops from 13.9–16.8 dB to 9.8–11.6 dB on 8 of 12 cells ([diag4](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/d24412a/docs/audits/song-settings-control-2026-10-15/diag4.out?plain=1#L1-L12)) [V]. The ladder places C′'s crest loss at **3.6–5.5 dB, mostly at timescales under 10 ms**. It also found that **quiet passages are 15–20 dB too loud**: frames 20–40 dB below the DI's peak sit only 6–8 dB below C′'s own peak ([ladder, L67–L78](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/d24412a/docs/rank-margin-ladder-results.md?plain=1#L64-L78)) [V]. So at "equal level", C′'s peaks are low and its quiet frames are high. The two errors point in opposite directions [I].

Input gain is the first stage of the project's chain model: "input gain → gate → compressor → drive → amp tone stack → …" ([`refchain.py` L9–L13](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/d24412a/analysis/refchain.py?plain=1#L5-L15)) [V]. That is the project's model, not a measurement of Morgan's binary. No committed check confirms it [V absence]. In the open NAM plugin, "input calibration" is literally a pre-gain: `inputGainDB = Input knob; if calibrating, inputGainDB += calibration level − model input level`, applied as one multiplier in front of the network ([NeuralAmpModeler.cpp](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sdatkinson/NeuralAmpModelerPlugin/main/NeuralAmpModeler/NeuralAmpModeler.cpp)) [V]. Amp-model training treats level as a primary condition. Damskägg et al. applied ±15 dB random gain during training because "the output signal sounds very different for different input levels" ([arXiv 1811.00334](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1811.00334)) [V]. This is the textbook Hammerstein ambiguity: the linear and nonlinear blocks are identifiable only "up to a scaling factor" ([Risuleo et al., arXiv 1412.4055](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1412.4055)) [U, search excerpt]. A DI level error of ε dB is therefore exactly an input-gain error of ε dB if input gain is a pure pre-gain, and that is a 2–4 render check (step 0) [I].

Put together, this gives a concrete hypothesis [I]. Matching transient bite through C′'s squashed peaks calls for more input gain. Matching the cleanliness of its too-loud quiet frames calls for less. The judge removes one global output level per window and then compares per-frame log-mel contours, scoring quiet frames fully under the recording's floor ([`aligned.py` L472–L479](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/d24412a/analysis/aligned.py?plain=1#L472-L479)) [V]. Which error wins depends on how much of a part is attack and how much is sustain, so the shift in the input-gain optimum has a part-dependent sign. The committed aggregate shows the average direction: C′'s objective prefers more input gain than the true one (mean ρ −0.09 against +0.17; sign agreement 3 of 13) ([a7c, L4](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/e623c41/docs/audits/arm-a-2026-10-16/levers-audit/a7c_dims.out?plain=1#L4)) [V]. So the squashed peaks win on average. Whether the sign flips by part is not in the committed file [I]. Shifts of a few dB are 0.1–0.15 in unit scale, the size of the search's fine steps. That is enough to invert local order along input gain. On the true side, an objective that runs monotonically to the floor makes this worse: a small rebuilt-side shift of the optimum into the interior reverses the order of every pair along that axis.

Three pieces of evidence argue against level being the *whole* story [V], and they lower but do not settle the expectation for experiment 2. An earlier network's "6 dB quieter" rebuild roughly halved the pick's under-drive bias, but "the closeness doesn't follow" ([underdrive-trim-results.md](../underdrive-trim-results.md), lines 8–40). An oracle-fitted static expander left the ladder's margin at 5 of 9 ([ladder](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/d24412a/docs/rank-margin-ladder-results.md?plain=1#L68-L72)). And of the two negative Cambridge parts, Prodigal's control rebuild lost about 4 dB of crest while just-don't-talk's was intact (13.9 → 13.6/13.8 dB) ([diag4](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/d24412a/docs/audits/song-settings-control-2026-10-15/diag4.out?plain=1#L3-L6)). Those diag4 rebuilds are of control fakes, not of the stems 2b used, so the comparison is suggestive only [I]. Every earlier "level costs little" result was measured on menu picks, and only integrated loudness was ever varied. Peak-referenced or quiet-frame-referenced calibration has never been tested, and nothing has been tested on local ranking ([set3-gap-split-results.md](../set3-gap-split-results.md), lines 18–46) [V/I].

## Only fixes that work without the true DI at deployment can help

Every fix below must work on a new song with no DI. Development data can teach a correction, but deployment cannot query the true objective [I]. That one constraint sorts the options.

**Level and drive calibration per part.** There are two forms, both cheap and both untested locally [I]. The *population* form sets each rebuild's gain so that its peak level (for example the 99.9th-percentile |x|, or the 1-ms peak) at −22.9 LUFS matches the median of the measure DIs from *other* bands. It costs zero renders to compute, and its rule is fixed before scoring. The *record-statistics* form is an indirect-inference set-up ([UCL JoE introduction](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10059813/1/JoE%20Indirect%20Inference%20-%20Introduction%20Nov%206%202017.pdf)) [U, search summary]. It fits a gain G and a one-parameter upward expander γ on a 20–50 ms envelope, so that the render of the corrected rebuild at S, and at 2–3 menu settings, matches the record's level-invariant statistics: frame-level quantiles relative to the median frame, per-band 5–10 ms crest, and the slope of spectral centroid against frame level. A 3 × 3 grid at 3 settings costs about 27 renders per part. The record still carries the amp-compressed image of the true dynamics, so matching *after the amp* is well posed even though matching the DI directly is not. The risk is that the statistics depend on the knobs too, which bakes S's own error into the fit [I]. No published method estimates absolute drive (DI level × input gain) from a post-cab record. Effect-parameter estimators such as Comunità et al.'s reach about 0.05 MAE on a 0–1 knob, but at fixed input levels ([arXiv 2012.03216](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.03216)) [V, abstract]. They estimate "drive at nominal level", the very product that cannot be separated here [I].

**Profiling out input gain, with a learned offset.** If the drive axis is unidentifiable, the objective should stop ranking it [I]. For a candidate θ, define f_prof(θ) as the minimum of the C′ objective over input gain g_S + δ, with δ in {−4, 0, +4} dB (±1/8 of the knob's range). Rank the other knobs on f_prof. Then set the reported input gain to g_S + δ̂ − b̂_g, where b̂_g is a learned C′ drive bias fitted on other bands and shrunk toward 0. The cost at search time is three renders per candidate instead of one. If the true judge is steep in input gain in the interior, as the pooled switch and gain-staging sensitivity suggests, this discards real signal along input gain. Profiling also changes how the tone knobs are ranked, because drive changes how EQ and mic sound. These are the two risks [I].

**A joint DI-correction search with priors.** Searching correction parameters together with the knobs means a render for every evaluated point, because each correction changes what enters a nonlinear amp. The gain ↔ input-gain degeneracy makes the joint problem rank-deficient without a normalisation ([parallel Wiener–Hammerstein, arXiv 1708.06543](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1708.06543)) [U, search excerpt]. Only a prior or a convention can break it. A second observation at a known drive, or the record's absolute level, would also do, but neither exists for a released song [I]. The practical form is a quadratic penalty that pulls the correction toward the true DIs' crest, quiet-spread and tilt distribution, with knobs shrunk toward S. The halfway-shrink result is, in Bayesian terms, evidence that a strong prior at S is right when the local likelihood is biased [I]. This is the most expensive fix (about 7.5k renders for a 52-render search on 142 directions), and it should come after experiment 2 shows which correction parameter matters.

**Matched degradation.** No audio or effect-estimation evidence shows that applying the same imperfect transform to both sides restores *local* rankings. A targeted search returned nothing relevant [V absence]. The degradation acts on the DI, which only the candidate side has. The record cannot be put through "the same C′ error" without the true DI. So only output-side transforms (smoothing, robust features, masks) can be applied symmetrically [I]. The fine-structure report's re-rebuild extrapolation, 2·L(r₁) − L(r₂) with r₂ rendered from C′'s rebuild of r₁, is a different idea. It was never scored locally ([di-rebuild-fine-structure-2026-10.md](di-rebuild-fine-structure-2026-10.md), "Score-side fixes are diagnostics…") [V]. With FLACs saved in experiment 1, the output-side variants and masks become zero-render re-scores [I].

**A learned correction or verifier, trained on paired development data.** Every standard multi-fidelity optimiser assumes the high-fidelity objective can be queried on the same problem. That covers co-kriging, BoTorch's `SingleTaskMultiFidelityGP`, rMFBO, and the Alexandrov trust region, whose first-order consistency needs "equal value and gradient at the center of the trust region" ([Peherstorfer et al. survey](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1806.10761)) [V]. These methods also degrade sharply when the low-fidelity source correlates only locally, and the case here is the reverse: globally right, locally wrong ([arXiv 2309.02771](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2309.02771)) [U]. At deployment the high-fidelity call costs infinity, so **only cross-part transfer works**. In that form, learn δ(θ − S; part features) from development pairs and apply it with zero true-DI calls [I]. The first candidate is a population-average gradient-bias correction, f̃(θ) = f_C′(θ) + b̂ᵀ(θ − S), ridge-shrunk toward 0. If the sign flips by band, b̂ must instead depend on 2–3 deployment-time features (C′'s crest deficit against the population, the record's level spread, class). The second candidate is a pairwise logistic ranker, in the spirit of ACM-ES's ranking surrogate ([Loshchilov et al. PPSN 2010](https://infoscience.epfl.ch/record/186020/files/PPSN2010_ACM-ES.pdf?version=1)) [U]. Both must be scored leave-one-band-out with nested selection and a shuffled-target control. Random cross-validation on grouped data badly understates error ([Roberts et al. 2017](https://www.biometrie.uni-freiburg.de/mitarbeiter/dormann/roberts-et-al-2017-ecography.pdf)) [V]. The pairs are dependent, so the effective sample is the number of bands, not 780 pairs per cell [I]. If a GP is ever used, the open options are BoTorch and GPyTorch (MIT) and Emukit and rMFBO (Apache-2.0). The original TuRBO code is non-commercial ("You may not use … outputs, or results from the Work … for commercial purposes") and can appear only as a flagged anchor. BoTorch's TuRBO tutorial is the MIT path ([BoTorch](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pytorch/botorch/main/LICENSE), [GPyTorch](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cornellius-gp/gpytorch/main/LICENSE), [Emukit](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/EmuKit/emukit/main/LICENSE), [TuRBO](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/uber-research/TuRBO/master/LICENSE.md)) [V]. This differs from the DI-free "same processing?" verifier in [song-to-settings-rethink-2026-10.md](song-to-settings-rethink-2026-10.md). That verifier needs about 10–11k new renders. The correction here is trained on (rebuilt, true) pairs the local sets already produce [I].

**The DAC codec route (E1′).** Step 0c round-tripped the 12 control cells' *measure* DIs through DAC 44 kHz with 4 and 9 codebooks and rendered S and T through each, 48 renders in all. E1′ opens only if the 9-codebook trip keeps m > 0 on all 4 fold-3 cells, keeps a mean m ≥ 0.75× the true DI's, and keeps 1-ms crest within 1 dB ([plan, L152–L172](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/d24412a/docs/di-fine-structure-plan.md?plain=1#L152-L172)) [V]. **The route passed.** On the 4 fold-3 cells (both Cambridge parts, on the PR12 and SW50R), the 9-codebook round trip keeps a mean margin of 0.168 against the true DI's 0.188 (bar 0.141, so 89%). Its worst 1-ms crest error is 0.013, and `opens` is true ([reading.json, L2–L12](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/e623c41/docs/audits/arm-a-2026-10-16/exp4-step0c-dac-reading.json?plain=1#L2-L12); [ledger](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/e623c41/docs/dev-test-ledger.json?plain=1#L376)) [V]. The shift test shows that `z` is not shift-stable at sample scale: its similarity drops to 0.80–0.84 after a one-sample delay, 0.57–0.67 after four and 0.33–0.46 after eight, on 6 parts ([shift.json](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/e623c41/docs/audits/arm-a-2026-10-16/exp4-step0c-dac-shift.json?plain=1#L1-L122)) [V]. A latent predictor would therefore be chasing a target that moves under a one-sample misalignment [I]. Per-cell m, the 4-codebook trip, the quiet-frame level, 5- and 10-ms crest and the resample-only control are not in the committed file [V absence]. A DAC round trip has been scored on menu picks (E0: 94% of oracle picks) and on that two-point margin, **never on a local set** ([egvae-lite plan](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/77d3f67/docs/egvae-lite-plan.md?plain=1#L125-L148)) [V]. C′ shows how coarse and local fidelity can come apart [I]. The route is a long shot. The arms that moved crest, gap and treble did not move local fidelity. Drive information destroyed in the amp is lost to a latent predictor too. And latent regression averages: about 2.0–2.6 mel against C′'s 2.29 ([E1 results](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/77d3f67/docs/egvae-e1-results.md?plain=1#L62-L70)) [V]. A judgement of **10–25%** that a trained E1′ lifts local Spearman to a usable level is reasonable [I]. The decisive cheap test is a **ceiling**: render the local settings through the true DI's own DAC-9 round trip. That costs about 600 renders on fold 3, or about 3.4k on the 14-band set. If that ceiling is not clearly above C′, no E1′ training is justified. Training cost is unmeasured, with a plausible range of one to three days of single-job MPS time [U].

## Hybrids keep the rebuild for coarse moves only

The strongest proven lever costs no extra search renders. Stop at the 52-render main stage and shrink halfway to S. That gives −0.083 [−0.135, −0.032], a worst band of +0.03 instead of +0.16, and 40 directions worse than S instead of 48 ([results, L43–L59](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/d24412a/docs/arm-a-levers-results.md?plain=1#L43-L59)) [V]. In trust-region terms, the rebuild is informative at the radius of the first 0.25 steps and uninformative at 1/8 and below. Shrinkage is a fixed-radius pull toward the prior mean S. It works because A's move has a positive cosine with K_S's on 92% of units while only about 0.36 of it lies along K_S's ([plan, L39–L45](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/d24412a/docs/arm-a-levers-plan.md?plain=1#L39-L45)) [V/I]. Every stored arm-A search replays exactly from its objective curve, so variants of the stop and shrink rules cost **one judge render per direction**: 142 plus 72 warm-ups [V]. Four variants are worth replaying [I]. The first resets input gain to S's value with the other knobs shrunk halfway. The second uses per-knob λ (0 for input gain, 0.5 elsewhere, mics kept as A's). The third accepts only moves found at step ≥ 1/8. The fourth stops the mic stage, to confirm that the mic gain comes from coarse categorical moves. Undoing A's mic changes "loses about half the gain" [V], which warns that removing a coarse move can cost real gain. Freezing input gain plausibly lands within ±0.02 of −0.083 [I]. Selecting among variants on the same 142 directions inflates the estimate, and the project's "choose on 13 bands, score the 14th" figure (−0.072) is the right correction [V].

**Arm B is at most a weak tie-breaker.** Ranking a stored K_S path by arm B's density prefers settings *farther* from the record (ρ +0.13 [+0.03, +0.24]). Only the song-specific part, the density relative to a song-blind baseline, has the right sign, and it is small: −0.07 [−0.12, −0.03] ([diagnostics, L49–L54](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/d24412a/docs/song-to-settings-diagnostics-results.md?plain=1#L49-L54)) [V]. In sound, blending toward B or toward a constant adds about nothing (−0.012, −0.011) [V]. The one zero-render use worth scoring is C′ + λ·(song-specific log-density ratio) on the local sets, with λ chosen leave-one-band-out. Expect −0.01 to −0.02 at most [I].

**Per-knob priors from optimum locations.** The committed evidence points away from generic location priors. The median of other parts' K_M optima is far *worse* than the menu preset: +0.229, 0 of 14 bands, and +0.452 on crunch ([knob-ceiling-results.md](../knob-ceiling-results.md), lines 89–90) [V]. A narrow, knob-specific rule is still worth one zero-render table. Build the per-knob K_S optimum distribution from the 144 stored K_S runs: share at each limit, by amp and class, and agreement between a part's two directions. It is not committed and costs nothing [V absence; I]. A floor rule for input gain would help only on runs whose optimum sits at the floor, and the input-gain floor may reflect the judge's taste rather than a record match ([knob-ceiling-results.md](../knob-ceiling-results.md), amendment 4 discussion around lines 76–90) [V/I].

## Three experiments on one Mac, measured as effect sizes

### The measurement contract

Every experiment reports estimates. None reports pass or fail. That follows the user's rule of 2026-10-16: learn from any improvement ([plan, L62–L97](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/d24412a/docs/arm-a-levers-plan.md?plain=1#L62-L97)) [V]. The **primary measure** is the band-weighted paired difference, fix minus C′, in local Spearman on frozen settings. Each cell's value is computed per half and averaged. Cells are averaged to parts, then to bands. The interval is the 90% band-clustered t(G − 1) from `song_readout.bw_stats` [V]. **Reliable-pair accuracy** is pre-declared as co-primary. It is the fraction of correctly ordered pairs among those whose true order agrees on both halves, and it strips the near-ties that pull any Spearman toward 0 [I]. The true DI's cross-half local Spearman (the ceiling) is reported beside every number, as a fraction as well as a level. **Secondary measures** come in this order. First is per-knob 3-way argmin agreement on each coordinate neighbourhood (−, 0, +), input gain first, then amp knobs, cab and switches. Next is local-move regret in log units: log d_true of the setting the objective picks among a neighbourhood, minus the neighbourhood's best. That mirrors exactly what `coord_search` does [I]. Last is the end-to-end held-half log ratio of the stopped-and-shrunk search, paired against −0.083. The part-weighted figure with a band sign-flip p, wins and losses, the leave-one-band-out range, and per-amp and per-class figures are descriptive [V rule].

Power comes from bands, not settings. At 40 settings per cell, the within-cell sampling SD of Spearman is about 0.16, already below the between-band spread implied by the stored interval [I, simulation]. The minimum detectable paired effect (80% power, 90% interval excludes 0) is **2.30 σ_d with 3 bands, 1.37 σ_d with 5 parts, and 0.71 σ_d with 14 bands** [I]. Here σ_d is the SD of band-mean paired differences. For local Spearman, the committed band means give σ_d of 0.06 (1z − C′), 0.08 (ensemble − C′) and 0.15–0.16 (1a and 1b − C′) ([a7b](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/e623c41/docs/audits/arm-a-2026-10-16/levers-audit/a7b_local.out?plain=1#L2-L6)) [I]. These are SDs of 3 numbers, so they are rough. Fold 3 alone could detect only Δρ ≈ 0.13–0.37, while 14 bands could detect about 0.04–0.11 [I]. For the end-to-end log ratio, the committed 3-C′-against-A interval [−0.031, +0.016] implies σ_d ≈ 0.05, so a correlated variant's MDE is about **0.035** [I]. **So the local set must leave fold 3.** It should cover all 72 (part, amp) cells and 14 bands, and keep render audio as 16-bit FLAC, which the project's rules allow ([ml-plan-2026-10.md](ml-plan-2026-10.md), line 497) [V]. Every later judge-side idea then costs no renders.

### Step 0: settle the plumbing (2–4 renders, about a day of analysis)

The first job is to **check that Morgan's input gain is a pure pre-gain**. On one fold-3 part and amp at S, render (x · 10^(ε/20), input gain g) against (x, input gain g + ε) for ε = ±6 dB. That is four renders, bypassing the LUFS normaliser. Compare the pairs sample by sample after alignment, and compare their judge distances against the 0.005-log repeat tolerance. If S has the gate or compressor on, keep them on, because a stage ahead of input gain would break exactness [I]. If the residual sits at render-noise level, DI level calibration and input-gain offsets become one experiment. The analysis side is then the zero-render diagnostics table above, run on the Mac's stored 2b, K_S and arm-A files. Step 0c's `reading.json` and `shift.json` are committed and read above. Only their per-cell m, DAC-4 and quiet-level figures remain on the Mac. **What to expect.** If the partial Spearman controlling input gain is clearly positive, experiment 2 leads with profiling. If the tone knobs are also inverted with a band-dependent sign, it leads with the learned correction and the reference decomposition. The committed marginal split already shows the sw50r's Mid, Level and Bass inverted (0 of 10), so the second branch is the likelier one [I].

### Experiment 1: a 14-band local set with saved audio and a four-way decomposition (about 7.1k renders, about 1.4 hours)

**Settings Σ, frozen in the plan with a new seed** [I]. Each of the 72 cells gets about 48 settings: S's ±1/8 coordinate neighbourhood (about 20–26 points), a seeded draw from both K_S directions to reach 40, and 8 points from arm A's own path. Without A's path points, the set is range-restricted to where the true search goes. **Renders.** Σ goes through C′ and through the measure DI, both saved as FLAC: 2 × 72 × 48 + 144 warm-ups ≈ 7.1k. At most 3 render processes run machine-wide ([plan, L288–L306](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/d24412a/docs/arm-a-levers-plan.md?plain=1#L288-L306)), and at that cap the label table ran about 5k renders per hour (6.5k in about 1 h 15 min, [label-table-results.md](../label-table-results.md), line 8; [diagnostics plan, L355](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/d24412a/docs/song-to-settings-diagnostics-plan.md?plain=1#L355-L356)). The 7k-per-hour figure is the 4-process rate ([song-settings plan, L265–L266](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/d24412a/docs/song-settings-plan.md?plain=1#L265-L266)). So experiment 1 takes about 1.4 hours, longer if other sessions' jobs share the cap [V caps and rates; I estimate]. **Scoring** costs only CPU time. It is the factorial over DI {C′, measure} × reference {stem, `reference.wav`} × lag {−52 − Δ, `judge_lag`, and 0} × mask {own DI, record}. Two corners reproduce known quantities, and both serve as checks. The C′/stem/−52/own-mask corner is the current objective, which should match 2b on fold 3. The measure/reference/judge_lag corner is the true d, which should match the stored values within 0.005. **Primary measure.** C′'s band-weighted local Spearman on 14 bands, with the ceiling. The decision-relevant contrast is the measure-DI-against-stem cell: the best any DI fix could reach with the only reference a song gives. If that cell is far below the ceiling, the reference is the bottleneck, and DI work should pause [I]. **Optional add-on:** the DAC-9 round trip of the measure DI as a third DI. That is about 600 renders on fold 3 as the minimal test, or about 3.4k on all cells, scored the same way and reported as a fraction of the measure-DI-against-stem cell. **Disk:** roughly 0.5–0.7 MB per 10-s mono FLAC, about 5 GB for this experiment, within the 30-GB-free rule [U, render length not checked].

### Experiment 2: level and drive calibration, and input-gain profiling (about 7–15k renders, 1.4–3 hours)

All variants go through C′ on experiment 1's frozen Σ, with FLAC saved, so each is paired with experiment 1's baseline [I]. **V1–V2** are constant DI offsets of ±4 dB, the same as input-gain shifts of ±1/8 if step 0 confirms a pure pre-gain (about 6.9k). They test the level hypothesis directly, and together with the baseline they give the 3-point input-gain profile f_prof at no further cost. **V3** is population peak calibration, a zero-render gain per part fixed by a rule set from other bands (about 3.5k). **V4** is record-statistics gain plus expander, with about 27 fitting renders per part and then Σ (about 4.1k). The full set is about 14.5k. A lean set of V1–V2 on the neighbourhood subset plus V3 is about 7k. The zero-render work on these renders includes the learned offset b̂_g (leave-one-band-out) and the profiled objective's Spearman. **Primary measure:** the band-weighted paired Δ local Spearman (and reliable-pair accuracy) of each variant against C′, with the ceiling. **Secondary measures:** input-gain argmin agreement, then the other knobs, local-move regret, and the coarse-pair Spearman (knob distance ≥ 0.25), to show that the coarse ranking has not been damaged. **What to expect.** The underdrive-trim and oracle-expander results set a modest prior. A Δρ of +0.1 to +0.3 would be a real lead, and anything above about +0.3 would cross the range where the literature says surrogates start to pay [I]. **Disk:** at experiment 1's rate per file, these FLACs add about 4–10 GB on top of experiment 1's 5 GB, so free space should be checked against the 30-GB rule before V1 starts [I].

### Experiment 3: hybrid pipelines end to end (0.9–8.5k renders)

**3a, at zero renders:** the learned gradient-bias correction (pooled, then with 2–3 part features) and the pairwise logistic ranker, both trained on experiment 1–2 pairs. Both are scored leave-one-band-out over 14 bands with nested selection, a shuffled-target control and the in-sample figure shown beside them. Also the arm-B tie-breaker [I]. **3b, about 0.9k renders:** the four replayed stop-and-shrink variants above, at 142 + 72 renders each, read against 3-C′-shrunk's −0.083. **3c, about 7.6k renders, only with the user's yes:** a live 52-render main stage with the best corrected objective from experiment 2 or 3a, shrunk halfway, judged on held halves on all 142 directions (142 × 53 + 72). It runs only if that objective's local Δρ estimate is positive. That is a routing rule, not a bar. **Primary measure:** the paired held-half log ratio against 3-C′-shrunk, with the MDE of about 0.035 stated in advance. Report "choose on 13 bands, score the 14th" alongside.

## Conclusion

The local failure is a measurement problem before it is a modelling problem. Three of the four differences between the rebuilt and true objectives (lag, mask and reference) cost nothing to separate once render audio is kept. One of them, the separated stem, may cap every DI fix at a level nobody has measured. That cap, the measure-DI-against-stem cell, is the most decision-relevant number the project lacks. The second is the true DI's own cross-half local stability, without which −0.126 cannot be placed. If both come back healthy and the partial Spearman shows input gain carrying the bias, the drive axis becomes a nuisance to profile out and calibrate, rather than a knob for the rebuild to rank. That is a cheap, deployable change. The committed marginal split already points that way for input gain (sign agreement 3 of 13), but also shows the sw50r's tone knobs inverted. If the partial confirms that the tone knobs are inverted too, the project's best answer remains the one it already has: the rebuild steers coarse moves for 52 renders, shrinkage holds the downside, and fine settings wait for an objective that has shown, on 14 bands, that it ranks neighbours.

The plan changes what a "good result" means. A fix that lifts local Spearman from −0.13 to +0.15 on 14 bands is a lesson about where the bias lives, even though it does not close the gap. Under the user's rule it should be reported as such, with its ceiling beside it, rather than discarded for missing a bar.
