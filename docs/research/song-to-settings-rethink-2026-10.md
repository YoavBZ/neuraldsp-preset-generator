# Score arm B's samples before rebuilding it

Evidence tags: **[V]** verified (a researcher opened the source, or it is in the repository's committed code or docs on `ml/cprime-folds` at `ded3667`); **[I]** inferred (reasoning or arithmetic from verified facts); **[U]** unverified (a search snippet, an estimate, or a file outside git). Links are written to work from `docs/research/`: repo docs as `../<file>.md`, code as `../../<path>`, other research docs as `<file>.md`, and branch-only files as GitHub links. Background on de-amping, proxies and the DI-recovery track is in [di-recovery-2026-10.md](di-recovery-2026-10.md) and [di-rebuild-fine-structure-2026-10.md](di-rebuild-fine-structure-2026-10.md) and is not repeated here.

**Bottom line.** Arm B did not fail only on songs. It failed on renders. Its positive control failed on all four folds, and when the planted targets were re-rendered through the measure DI, so that they were reachable, it closed half the gap on **1 of 33 units** ([results, L44–L68](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/ml/cprime-folds/docs/song-settings-results.md?plain=1#L44-L68)) [V]. So the render-to-record gap cannot be the main cause. The hypothesis "each knob is ill-posed from sound, so the model regresses to the middle" fits the inverse-synthesis literature, where maximum-likelihood regressors are known to average across equivalent settings ([Hayes et al. 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.07199)) [V]. It does not explain this failure on its own. The project's exact-copy control shows that wrong but compensating settings already close about 55–80% of the gap (the adopted search, 4 cells on 2 parts; the plan's prose gives 65–80%) [I, from [knob-ceiling-plan.md](../knob-ceiling-plan.md), lines 225–234]. A model that output any member of that "sounds-alike" set would pass the 50% bar. Arm B's failure therefore needs averaging on top of ill-posedness, an MDN that collapsed, or an input that hides the knobs. The rival explanations (MDN collapse, the song pipeline, a lossy input, label sampling) can be told apart for about **8k renders and no new training**: sample the existing MDN on fresh planted targets rendered through the measure DI, from a clean and a song-like input view, and render jitters around each true setting so that "sounds alike" is measured directly ("E-cov" plus a truth-jitter test). Two follow-ups depend on its answer. The first is an isolated-DI retrain (no new renders for training, about 2.2k to score). The second is either a learned "same processing?" verifier (about 16k renders) or sound-space training of the sampler (about 12k). The worst path costs about 30k renders, taking the committed ~143k to about 173k of the 200k limit. A menu ranker and descriptor prediction rank last: the knob ceiling showed that generating settings beats ranking the menu, and descriptor prediction was already tried (ROADMAP 8c).

## Arm B failed on renders, and the record says so

The committed step-3 readout scores arm B at **+0.035 [−0.038, +0.108] against S**, better in 6 of 14 bands, and no better than a song-blind constant (−0.009 [−0.069, +0.052]) ([results, L19–L24, L47–L48](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/ml/cprime-folds/docs/song-settings-results.md?plain=1#L19-L48)) [V]. The shuffled-input control passes (−0.074, 12 of 14 bands), so the model reads its input. The full bank is no better than the half bank (+0.017), so the reading is "failed", not "data-limited" [V]. The audit found the pipeline correct: feature parity, 0 label mismatches over 28,813 values, consistent encoding, and no collapse onto S. On held-out bank renders, though, the model's mean knob error is 0.22–0.23 against 0.23–0.25 for the median. Per-setting correlations are 0.1–0.6, mic accuracy is about 0.4, and prediction spread is about a third of label spread ([L53–L62](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/ml/cprime-folds/docs/song-settings-results.md?plain=1#L53-L62)) [V]. For uniform labels on [0, 1], always predicting 0.5 gives an expected error of 0.25, so the model extracts almost no per-knob information on average [I].

The research notes disagree on one fact, and the committed record settles it. One note says arm B's "failure mode is not recorded in the repository" and ranks its options on the assumption that only the real-song gate failed. That is wrong for commit `32c9899` and later. The positive control (planted settings with a known answer) **fails on all 4 folds**. It closes ≥ 50% of the gap on 27–35 of 53–63 units, against a two-thirds rule. It is closer than the bank median on 39–48 units, which would pass [V]. The audit then found a defect. The planted targets had been rendered through the raw DI, while the bank used the measure DI ([`song_armb.py` L84–L94](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/ml/cprime-folds/learn/song_armb.py?plain=1#L84-L94)) [V]. Re-rendered through the measure DI (17 targets), "the control fails harder: 1 of 33 units close half the gap" [V]. No fix-and-re-run was used. **The committed fact is that arm B cannot recover reachable, planted settings from bank-like audio.** That moves render-domain fixes (the loss, the head, the input) up the ranking and moves "find a scorer for a good posterior" down, until a test shows the posterior is good.

The failed test was not a pure inversion test. Its inputs were mixed, mastered and separated as in the bank ([plan L196–L205](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/ml/cprime-folds/docs/song-settings-plan.md?plain=1#L196-L205)) [V]. So "the model can't invert the plugin" and "the song pipeline destroys the cues" are still confounded. Two other gaps matter. Arm B's audit artefacts (per-setting correlations by name, top-component weights, the share of predictions near S) live under `~/ndsp-presets/learn/song-settings/readout/audit/` on the Mac and are not in git [V that they are uncommitted]. The arm's actual epoch time was never committed either; only the ML plan's estimate of 0.7–2.6 h per fold exists ([ml-plan-2026-10.md](ml-plan-2026-10.md), lines 351–353) [V].

The model itself is a 4-block residual CNN over a 2 × 96 × 446 log-mel (stem and mix, one 4.5-s half), pooled to the mean and standard deviation over time. It feeds a 512-512 MLP with a 4-component diagonal Gaussian MDN over the 17-setting continuous union, 6 switch logits and two 10-way mic heads. Its single answer is the heaviest component's mean ([`song_model.py` L82–L113, L256–L276](../../learn/song_model.py#L82-L113)) [V]. The bank is half uniform-broad and half jittered (σ 0.1) around S or same-fold K_S optima. The window comes from the fold's own DIs with p 0.5, otherwise from the shared Guitar-TECHS P1/P2 pool ([`song_bank.py` L220–L268](../../learn/song_bank.py#L220-L268)) [V]. Each render is stored as a peak-normalised 16-bit FLAC with its full settings and peak. Each example records its DI window (path and start), and that window can be regenerated exactly with `measure(load_window(...))`. Mixes and stems are kept only as float16 log-mel features ([`song_bank.py` L300–L335](../../learn/song_bank.py#L300-L335); [`song_aug.py` L192–L205](../../learn/song_aug.py#L192-L205)) [V]. Fold models are saved as `fold{f}/model.pt` ([`song_model.py` L231–L244](../../learn/song_model.py#L231-L244)) [V], so they can be sampled again without retraining. That they still exist on the Mac is [U].

## "Many settings sound alike" fits the literature but cannot carry this failure alone

The literature supports the hypothesis in general form. Hayes, Saitis and Fazekas state that sound-to-parameter inversion "is an inherently ill-posed problem" and that maximum-likelihood regression "is minimized by a suboptimal averaging" across equivalent solutions. In their Surge XT tests, a VAE + RealNVP "consistently collapsed to predicting average values for many parameters" ([Hayes et al. 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.07199)) [V]. FlowSynth shows how far parameter error and audio error can come apart. On 32 Diva parameters, a CNN has a *better* parameter error than a regression flow (0.159 vs 0.193) but about 21× worse spectral convergence (19.18 vs 0.911) ([Esling et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1907.00971)) [V]. Masuda and Saito found that "parameter loss is an inadequate criterion for match quality" ([ISMIR 2021](https://archives.ismir.net/ismir2021/paper/000053.pdf)) [V]. Bishop's original case for the MDN is that least-squares nets "approximate the conditional averages", which are not valid answers to multi-valued inverse problems ([Bishop 1994](https://publications.aston.ac.uk/id/eprint/373/)) [V]. MDNs themselves "suffer from instabilities in training and mode collapse" ([Makansi et al. 2019](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.03631)) [V]. Arm B's symptoms match this picture: spread at a third of the labels, error near the median's, and no gain from more data. The earlier PR12 proof of concept showed the same shrinkage, at 0.44 of label spread ([preset-model-poc-results.md](../preset-model-poc-results.md)) [V].

The guitar-specific evidence cuts the other way. Every amp or distortion estimator with good numbers works on one effect, 2–3 knobs and isolated plugin renders. Jürgens et al. report a distortion gain MAE of 0.05, tone 0.10 and the perceptually weak "edge" 0.14 ([DAFx-20](http://dafx2020.mdw.ac.at/proceedings/papers/DAFx2020_paper_2.pdf)) [V, via an earlier project note]. Comunità et al. report a gain MAE of 0.040 and tone 0.038 across 13 overdrive and fuzz plugins, conditioned on the true effect class ([arXiv 2012.03216](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.03216)) [V, via an earlier project note]. For a single clipper, Peladeau and Peeters found that plain parameter loss beat an audio loss on *both* parameter and audio error. The ranking reversed only for the whole EQ → compressor → clipper chain ([arXiv 2310.11781](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.11781)) [V]. Mixing alone degrades estimates: Hinrichs et al. estimated effects from instrument mixtures and saw error grow with mix level ([EURASIP 2022](https://asmp-eurasipjournals.springeropen.com/articles/10.1186/s13636-022-00257-4)) [V, via an earlier project note]. No published work estimates more than five amp-sim knobs from a mix or a record [V, absence in the searches]. The Morgan setup (9–11 interacting continuous settings per amp, 17 across the three amps, with pre- and post-gain tone shaping, a backing mix and separation) sits well outside the regime with published numbers. The literature therefore makes ill-posedness plausible, not proven [I].

The project's own evidence points the same way, with one important twist. In the exact-copy control, each knob moved alone shows a smooth V down to zero, "but the settings trade off (input gain, volume, treble, mic, cab position)". A search that moves a few knobs at a time "stalls at about 20–35% of the start distance, with the wrong settings compensating". It recovers 1–4 of 5 knobs within 0.1 ([knob-ceiling-plan.md](../knob-ceiling-plan.md), lines 225–234) [V]. Optima are also part-specific: a part's own searched settings beat other parts' by −0.434 in 14 of 14 bands. And 93% of searches end with some setting at a range limit ([knob-ceiling-results.md](../knob-ceiling-results.md)) [V]. Here is the twist. When the exact copy has d_target = 0, stalling at 20–35% of the start distance means **wrong-but-compensating settings close 65–80% of the gap** [I]. The amendment's own table is a little wider: the adopted v5 search ends at 0.19–0.45 of the start distance, so 55–81% closed, on 4 cells from 2 parts; these are end points of a judge-optimised search from S moved by 5 knobs, not a measured volume of equal-score settings ([knob-ceiling-plan.md](../knob-ceiling-plan.md), lines 217–223) [V]. The positive control asks for only 50%. A model whose single answer landed anywhere in that equal-score set would pass. Ill-posedness predicts a broad set of good answers, not the absence of good answers. Arm B's 1 of 33 needs a second cause: it averages *between* members of the set into a worse middle, its posterior has collapsed to one wide component, or its input does not carry the cues. Range-limit optima make the averaging story concrete. A model that shrinks toward 0.5 is structurally far from settings that sit at 0 or 1 [I].

### Seven rival explanations, and what rules each out

The symptoms (shrinkage, near-median knob error, no data gain) are the same whether the plugin is ill-posed or the input is uninformative. Shrinkage cannot by itself separate "ill-posed plugin" from "uninformative input" [I]. Each rival needs its own test. Most tests cost nothing or reuse one shared batch of renders.

| Explanation | What it predicts | Cheapest discriminating test | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ill-posed knobs plus averaging | MDN *samples* often close ≥ 50% of the gap while the top-component mean does not; many renders far from the truth in knob space still close the gap | E-cov: oracle best-of-K of MDN samples on fresh, reachable targets; plus 8 jitters around each true setting | Inside experiment 1 |
| MDN collapse (Makansi) | Effective number of components ≈ 1; predicted σ near the label spread; top mean ≈ mixture mean | Read π, σ and μ on held-out bank features; PIT/SBC per knob | 0 renders, minutes |
| Backing mix, mastering, separation | A clean-input view succeeds where the song view fails | E-cov with two input views of the same targets; then an isolated-DI retrain | Experiments 1 and 2 |
| Lossy input (96-band log-mel, mean/std time pooling) | Knobs move the judge's features clearly (sharp valley), yet even a clean-input model cannot invert | Knob-sensitivity test (one-knob moves, feature Jacobian); isolated retrain still fails | ~1k renders + experiment 2 |
| Label sampling (uniform broad half, off the region real optima occupy) | Errors concentrate on broad-drawn targets or at range limits | Split experiment 1's targets half broad, half drawn around other folds' K_S optima | 0 extra |
| Level-only settings | Knob error inflated on settings that only change level, which the judge removes | Sensitivity test: Δjudge ≈ 0 for those settings | Inside the sensitivity test |
| Render-to-record gap | Hurts songs, not renders | Already ruled out as the *main* cause: the render-domain control fails | — |

Two of these deserve a note. The DiffMoog gradient test found that mel-spectrogram losses point toward the true oscillator frequency only at chance (about 0.45–0.52 of 1,000 trials), against 0.64–0.75 for linear-spectrogram variants ([arXiv 2401.12570](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.12570v1)) [V]. PNP found the Jacobian metric of its feature map to be rank 2–4 ([arXiv 2301.02886](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.02886)) [V]. So identifiability depends on the feature map, not only on the plugin. But the judge itself uses a 64-band log-mel ([`aligned.py` L1–L46](../../analysis/aligned.py#L1-L46)) [V]. A cue that 96 mel bands lose is mostly invisible to the score too. The more plausible feature loss is arm B's pooling of mean and standard deviation over time, which discards the moment-to-moment texture the judge compares frame by frame [I]. Second, input gain is defined against a DI fixed at −22.9 LUFS, and the mix is renormalised before the model sees it, so only drive *character* carries input gain to the model [I, from [`song_bank.py`](../../learn/song_bank.py#L287-L297) and [`song_aug.py`](../../learn/song_aug.py)]. Whether any Morgan setting acts as a pure output level was not checked against the manual [U]. If one does, it is unidentifiable by construction, like Hayes's "zero global gain" example, and harmless in sound.

## Seven routes to a working arm B, and which fit the Mac

The sound-space literature agrees on a pattern. Pure audio-loss training is fragile. Parameter pre-training followed by an audio loss, or a parameter loss weighted by perceptual sensitivity, works better. Sampling candidates and rescoring them in audio beats a single regressed point. The constraint the project adds is the scorer: rescoring needs the take's DI, and the DI route is blocked by C′ ([DI recovery](di-recovery-2026-10.md)) [V].

### Sound-space training helps only inside the render domain

On the bank, the DI is known. A training loss can therefore compare render(DI, θ̂) with render(DI, θ) on the same notes, the regime where even hand-made distances work (own-DI regret about 0.035–0.04) ([di-free-distance-results.md](../di-free-distance-results.md)) [V]. At inference the predictor still reads only the stem and the mix. **The DI constraint bites only if a proxy refines settings at test time**, and then the only DI is C′ [I]. Keeping the proxy training-only is exactly DeepAFx-ST's NP-HH configuration, its worst method [V]. Gradients through a neural proxy are the riskiest version. The project's earlier settings-to-curve forward model erred by 3.5–4.5 dB per band against 4.1–5.3 dB for a constant curve, and choosing through it lost to template+R ([preset-model-poc-results.md](../preset-model-poc-results.md)) [V]. The render-in-the-loop alternative that fits the budget is not gradient-based. Sample K settings from the model for each of 1–2k bank examples, render them through the example's own DI, judge each against the stored render, and fine-tune with reward-weighted NLL or a best-of-K relabel. That is DDSynth-RL's pattern scaled down, and its in-domain loss is the warning [I]. None of these fixes a render-to-record gap. They fix averaging and wasted capacity on inaudible knobs [I].

### Posteriors need a scorer the project lacks

Arm B's MDN is already a weak neural posterior estimator that was never sampled [V]. The cheapest posterior test is to sample it, not to replace it. Coverage arithmetic sets K. If a "good" region holds posterior mass p, then K samples hit it at least once with probability 1 − (1 − p)^K. That needs K = 11, 22 or 45 for a 90% chance at p = 0.2, 0.1 or 0.05 [I]. A better head is worth building only if the MDN's samples miss while the bank shows the region is learnable. The SBI literature adds a caution: under simulator misspecification, "naive NPE gives misleading and erratic posteriors" ([Ward et al. 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.06564)) [V]. The bank's prior is an arbitrary mixture (half uniform, a quarter around S, a quarter around K_S optima), and a posterior model inherits it. Sequential, locally dense sampling beats amortised sampling for sample efficiency ([Lueckmann et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.04653)) [V]. That argues for sampling near S and K_S optima, as the jittered half already does.

### A learned "same processing?" verifier is the only DI-free scorer with a real training signal, and its odds are low

Without a DI, the project has tested four scorers, and none works. The judge needs the take's DI. C′'s rebuild ranks the true target below S on 7 of 12 known-target cells, and searching through it closes the gap on 3 of 9 informative cells against 9 of 9 for the measure DI ([song-settings-plan.md](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/ml/cprime-folds/docs/song-settings-plan.md?plain=1#L340-L355)) [V]. All eight hand-made DI-free distances failed. Their regret was 0.32–0.46 against 0.42 for the song-blind constant. The same distances reach 0.035–0.04 with the part's own DI, because "the performance … moves these distances more than the preset does" ([di-free-distance-results.md](../di-free-distance-results.md)) [V]. The log-mel and PANNs rerankers failed, and so did the contrastive tone encoder ([ROADMAP](../ROADMAP.md)) [V]. A long-term spectrum caps any spectral-statistics scorer: a perfectly known long-term curve bought about 5%, against about 20% for choosing by the judge ([preset-model-poc-results.md](../preset-model-poc-results.md)) [V].

The verifier differs from those failures in three ways. It is trained *on the exact question*, in song conditions, with different-DI pairs. Input A is a bank example's stored song-side features (stem and mix, already cached). Input B is a candidate setting θ_b rendered through a fixed probe DI. The target is the judge's distance between render(DI_i, θ_b) and the stored render(DI_i, θ_a), which is computable because DI_i is known [I]. At inference it needs K probe renders per song and no song DI. Different-content pairs were the single largest lever in RelFx (removing them raised L_d from 1.399 to 1.876) ([RelFx](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.10573)) [V, prior note]. The bank as stored lacks these pairs, because each setting was rendered through exactly one DI [V]. So the verifier needs new renders: one label render through the anchor's DI and one probe render per candidate. Sharing a candidate pool brings this to about 10–11k renders for 1.5k anchors × 6 candidates [I]. Its risk is the tone encoder's: strong on renders, useless on records [V]. With a weak scorer, a large K hurts. Picking the best of K noisy scores selects scorer error, so K should stay at 8–16 unless the scorer reaches a Spearman of about 0.6 [I].

The label table gives the verifier a free real-domain gate. It holds 59 clean and crunch development parts, each with a stem and the judge's scores for all 111 menu presets on 3 amps through the measure DI ([label-table-results.md](../label-table-results.md)) [V]. Rendering the 111 presets × 3 amps through the probe DI once (333 renders) lets the verifier rank each part's menu from its stem. That ranking can then be scored with the DI-free-distance gate already declared: regret at most 0.75× the song-blind constant's, better than the constant across bands, and agreement with the judge of at least 0.6 ([di-free-distance-results.md](../di-free-distance-results.md)) [V gate; I reuse]. C′ as a *relative* scorer among nearby proposals is also untested. But if experiment 4 produces a rebuild that passes the known-target control, the judge through that rebuild becomes the scorer, and arm C opens without a verifier [I]. **So the verifier should wait for experiment 4's outcome.**

### The menu ranker is a proposer, not an answer

A ranker over 111 audibly distinct presets avoids inaudible directions, and the label table supplies real-stem supervision. That is why one note ranked it first. But the knob ceiling found "the menu was never near the ceiling … Predicting settings, not ranking presets, is the direction from here". The true-DI search beats the best menu preset by −0.162 in 14 of 14 bands ([knob-ceiling-results.md](../knob-ceiling-results.md)) [V]. A menu pick therefore needs refinement, and refinement needs the scorer above. The DI-free menu rerankers already failed ([ROADMAP](../ROADMAP.md), "DI-free reranker, 2026-10-07") [V], and 59 parts in few crunch bands is thin supervision. It remains a zero-render side test, worth running only as a proposer for arm C [I].

## Measure sound, ladder the tests, and spend songs last

Knob error is not a gate. The ML plan says so: "Judge a settings model by knob error | Many settings sound alike; judge the sound" ([ml-plan-2026-10.md](ml-plan-2026-10.md), line 543) [V]. The Surge XT inversion paper drops parameter metrics as "uninformative at best and misleading at worst" ([Hayes et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.07199)) [V]. Automatic audio metrics agree with listening only partly: MSS and parameter loss each ranked differently from listeners in 3 of 16 cases, even on 2-parameter synths ([Salimi et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.22628v2)) [V]. Listening therefore stays the guard on the leader, as the plan already requires [V].

The proposed ladder has four rungs. **Rung 0 (zero renders, never gating)** reads the model: knob MAE and per-knob correlation against the median, restricted to knobs the sensitivity test shows are audible; the MDN's effective component count, σ and top-vs-mixture distance; PIT histograms per knob (an SBC-style check, [Talts et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.06788)) [V]; TARP on the joint vector ([sbi guide](https://sbi.readthedocs.io/en/latest/how_to_guide/17_tarp.html)) [V]; and reliability diagrams for mics and switches. Calibration is a diagnostic, never a pass rule. A calibrated posterior equal to the prior passes SBC [I]. Point estimators trained with MSE or MAE have marginals "strictly narrower than the truth" whenever the posterior has width ([Baattrup et al. 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22891)) [V], which is arm B's one-third spread.

**Rung 1 (fresh held-out bank-like targets, reachable)** is the corrected positive control. Each planted setting is rendered through the example's **measure-treated** DI window, so d_target ≈ 0 and the defect cannot recur. The targets come from DI windows the fold's model never saw. Model input comes in two views: (1a) the bank's lightest path (backing, no mastering, no separation) and (1b) the full song path (mastered, separated with htdemucs_6s). Every candidate is rendered through the same window and judged at lag 0. The model reads the search half; the judge scores the held half. The scores are gap closed, (d_S − d_cand)/d_S, and log ratios against S, the bank median and the song-blind constant. A best-of-K curve for K = 1…8 (or 16) is picked on the search half and scored on the held half, beside the oracle pick. That curve sits next to two baselines: K random bank-label draws and K jitters around S (σ 0.1). Min-over-K improves with K even for an uninformed sampler. The same bias is documented in trajectory forecasting, where minADE "become[s] increasingly biased" as k grows ([review](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.14831)) [V, secondary]. So the model must beat both baselines at matched K. Fresh seeds per iteration mean no repeat-use cost. They are still entered in the ledger.

**Rung 2 (known-target control, fresh draw)** uses arm A's design: S with five realistic moves, played through the part's **raw** DI, scored through the measure DI with d_target measured. A cell is informative if d_S − d_target ≥ 0.1 ([plan, amendment 2](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/ml/cprime-folds/docs/song-settings-plan.md?plain=1#L312-L322)) [V]. The original 12 cells are on their third use, and experiment 4's step 5 claims a further fresh 12 cells, from 6 bands drawn out of 8, with `random.Random(20261019)` ([ledger L362–L374](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/ml/cprime-folds/docs/dev-test-ledger.json?plain=1#L362-L374)) [V]. Between them, those two draws can touch up to 12 of the 14 step-3 bands. So arm B's fresh cells should come from classed development parts outside both sets: development has 34 bands ([song-settings-folds.json](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/ml/cprime-folds/docs/song-settings-folds.json)) [V], and the label table lists 59 clean or crunch parts. Any shortfall is entered as an nth use [I]. **Rung 3 (songs, last)** reuses step 3's 24 parts × 3 amps × 2 directions with step 3's rule unchanged: ≥ 0.03 better than S and ≥ 40% of K_S's band-weighted gain (about −0.108), ≥ 60% of bands, and the 0.05 class harm guard ([plan L227–L237](https://github.com/YoavBZ/neuraldsp-preset-generator/blob/ml/cprime-folds/docs/song-settings-plan.md?plain=1#L227-L237)) [V; −0.108 is I]. Two conditions are added. "Better than the song-blind constant" becomes a pass condition, because it is what made step 3 null [I]. The shuffled-input control applies to any single-shot model. This rung is a repeat use and is ledgered.

### Experiment 1: E-cov, truth-jitter and sensitivity, on the existing models (about 8k renders, no training)

This experiment answers the diagnosis without training anything. **Step 1.0 (0 renders, CPU, about an hour)** reads the four saved fold models on held-out bank features. It reports rung 0's collapse and calibration figures, and it commits the per-setting correlations the audit left outside git. In the same step, it replays the stored K_S searches. `coord_search` is seeded and deterministic, and `summary()` stores every evaluation's (A, B) distances in order ([`knob_ceiling.py` L135–L153, L495–L508](../../learn/knob_ceiling.py#L135-L153)) [V]. So feeding the stored values back should regenerate every evaluated settings vector. The replay counts only if it reproduces each stored best vector exactly [I; replay untested, and the stored runs live on the Mac, U]. If it does, the knob spread of settings within 0.03 and 0.1 of each cell's best held-half distance measures "equally good answers" on real records for free.

**Step 1.1 (about 7k renders, about 1 h)** draws 30 fresh planted targets per fold, 120 in all, with a declared new seed. Half are drawn like the bank's broad half. The other half are drawn around other folds' K_S optima (σ 0.1), so the song-like prior is tested too. The DI windows come from the fold's own bands, which the fold-f model never saw because it trains on `fold != f` rows ([`song_model.py` L166–L180](../../learn/song_model.py#L166-L180)) [V], plus unused P1/P2 windows. Each target gets about 58 renders through its measure-DI window:

| Candidate | Renders |
|---|---|
| The planted setting (the target) and S | 2 |
| View 1a: top-component mean + 4 MDN samples, per direction | 10 |
| View 1b: top mean + 8 MDN samples, per direction; mixture mean per direction | 20 |
| Bank median and song-blind constant | 2 |
| 8 random bank-label draws; 8 jitters around S (σ 0.1) | 16 |
| 8 jitters around the planted truth (σ 0.1, mics kept with p 0.7) | 8 |

Separation for view 1b takes about 10 minutes of CPU [I, scaled from the plan's ~7 h per 5k]. The judge's CPU cost per call is undocumented and should be timed first [U]. **Step 1.2 (about 1k renders)** is the knob-sensitivity test. On 10 held-out windows × 4 base points (S and 3 planted settings), it renders the base plus each continuous setting moved 0.15 toward the interior, and each switch flipped. That gives per-setting Δjudge and a finite-difference Jacobian of the judge's log-mel frames, whose eigen-spectrum exposes compensating directions, as PNP's metric did [I]. An optional 1k renders adds kNN-8 from the bank in arm B's trunk embedding, a zero-training proposer to compare against MDN samples.

The rules below are declared before the renders. A unit is a (target, direction) pair. It is informative if d_S ≥ 0.1 on the held half, and the target's repeat render must stay within 1e-3 [I proposals, built on the project's floors].

| Reading | Rule (every fold) | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| R1, single shot (the corrected positive control) | Top-component mean closes ≥ 50% on ≥ 2/3 of informative units **and** is strictly closer than the bank median on ≥ 2/3 | Arm B works as a one-shot predictor in the render domain (expected to fail, given 1 of 33) |
| R2, coverage | Best-of-K MDN samples (K = 8 on view 1b, K = 4 on view 1a), picked on the search half, close ≥ 50% on ≥ 2/3 **and** beat best-of-K random and best-of-K jitter-S (the first K draws of each) by ≥ 0.03 mean log ratio | The posterior holds good answers; what is missing is a picker |
| R3, pipeline | R1 or R2 passes on view 1a but fails on 1b | Backing, mastering or separation is the main loss |
| R4, ill-posed at the control's bar | ≥ 1/3 of all renders that close ≥ 50% lie ≥ 0.2 (mean per-knob) from the truth, **and** truth-jitters close ≥ 50% on ≥ 2/3 | A broad equal-score set exists; averaging explains R1's failure |
| R4′, well-posed | < 10% far-but-good renders, **and** gap closed falls steeply with knob distance (Spearman ≤ −0.5) | A sharp valley; arm B's 0.22 knob error is the failure, so the model is weak |

The readings also cover the remaining rivals. Effective components near 1 in step 1.0 flags MDN collapse. A target-type split (broad vs K_S-prior) flags label sampling. Δjudge ≈ 0 for a setting flags it as level-only or inaudible, and that setting is dropped from every knob diagnostic. If R2 passes on view 1b, the conditional **step 1.3** runs: oracle best-of-16 on step 3's 144 real directions with the true DI (about 2.3k renders, a ledgered repeat use). That shows whether the posterior also covers records.

### Experiment 2: the isolated-DI retrain (0 renders to train, about 2.2k to score, 3–10 MPS hours)

This experiment separates "cannot invert the plugin" from "the song pipeline hides the cues" more cleanly than view 1a can. The existing models always saw a backing in the mix channel [V, from `song_aug.py`]. The bank's clean FLACs and peaks are stored [V]. So clean-render log-mel features (stem and mix both set to the clean render at its true level) can be computed on the CPU in minutes [I]. The same architecture and recipe are then retrained on four folds. The time is 0.7–2.6 h per fold by the ML plan's uncommitted estimate [U], and the training queues behind experiment 4 on MPS. Scoring reuses experiment 1's 120 targets with the clean view: per direction, the top mean plus 8 samples, so about 18 renders per target and about 2.2k in all [I]. **The pass rule is R1 and R2 on clean input, every fold.**

The readings lead to different next actions. If the clean retrain passes R1, the model can invert from clean audio, and the song pipeline is the loss. Arm B then waits on separation and on DI-side work, and the next model step is song-robustness (for example, distilling a clean-trained teacher into a song-view student), not a new head [I]. If it passes R2 only, a proposer-plus-scorer design is right (experiment 3A). If it fails both and experiment 1 read R4′ (well-posed), the front end is the suspect. A follow-up arm would drop the time pooling or use a higher-resolution input. It ranks low, because the judge itself is a 64-band log-mel [I]. If it fails both and experiment 1 read R4 (ill-posed), a sampler needs sound-space training (experiment 3B). If experiment 1 fell between R4 and R4′, read experiment 2 on its own: passing R1 or R2 decides as above, and failing both sends the next step to 3B with the front-end follow-up as its control [I].

### Experiment 3: the scorer or the sampler, chosen by experiments 1–2

Exactly one branch runs. Both stop at the same end gates: a fresh known-target draw (rung 2), then songs (rung 3).

**3A: the "same processing?" verifier** runs if R2 passes on view 1b (or after experiment 2's R2-only reading), **and** experiment 4 has not produced a passing rebuild. A passing rebuild would make the judge itself the scorer. Training uses 1.5k anchors from training folds × 6 candidates. Candidates come from a shared pool at graded knob distances, including the anchor's own settings as a d = 0 positive. That costs about 9k label renders through anchor DIs plus about 1.5k probe renders, with a pairwise ranking loss on cached features. The MPS time is a few hours [U]. It has three gates, read in order. **G1 (render domain, ~1.9k probe renders):** on experiment 1's targets, already judged, its pick among the 8 MDN samples closes ≥ 50% on ≥ 2/3 of informative units and beats a random pick among the same 8 by ≥ 0.03, every fold. **G2 (real domain, 333 renders):** on the 59 label-table stems, menu regret ≤ 0.75× the song-blind constant's, better than the constant across bands, and judge agreement ≥ 0.6. That is the DI-free gate, reused and ledgered as a repeat use of those parts. **G3 (fresh known-target cells, ~1.5k renders including a 50-render true-DI yardstick search per cell-direction):** the verifier's pick among B's samples closes ≥ 50% on ≥ 2/3 of informative cells and is closer than the constant on ≥ 2/3. With fewer than 6 informative cells, the mean shortfall must be within 0.03 of the yardstick's. If the yardstick itself fails row 1, the setup is wrong; stop. Total: about **14.5k renders, plus about 1.3k for songs**. The prior odds are low [I]. The project's DI-free scorers have all lost to the constant once the notes differ [V], and FXRepBench's best learned scorer recovered only about half the oracle's edge on easier data [V, prior note].

**3B: sound-space training of the sampler** runs if R2 fails on both views and experiment 2 fails, under the R4 reading. It comes in two steps. First, if step 1.0 shows collapse, swap the MDN head for a conditional flow head (zuko NSF, MIT) on cached features: 0 renders to train, 1–2 h per fold [U]. If not, run the render-in-the-loop relabel: 1k bank examples × K = 8 samples through each example's DI = 8k renders, then a reward-weighted NLL fine-tune. Either way, R1 and R2 are re-scored on fresh experiment-1-style targets (about 2.2k). Then G3's fresh known-target cells follow (about 1.5k). Total: about **12k**. DDSynth-RL's in-domain loss is the warning to watch: a sharper sound-space sampler can lose calibration [V].

## Conclusion

The step-3 readout framed arm B's failure as a property of the problem: knobs are ill-posed from sound. The evidence supports a narrower and more useful claim. The equal-score sets the project has already measured *appear broad enough to pass the positive control on 4 cells*, which E-cov and the truth-jitter test check directly; so ill-posedness should have helped a sampler, not sunk it. What failed is a single averaged answer from a model that, in-domain, barely beats the median. That shifts the first question from "which new architecture?" to "do the existing model's samples already contain good answers, and through which input view?" One batch of about 8k renders answers it, and each answer points to a different owner. Coverage without a picker points to the scorer, and so to experiment 4 or the verifier. Clean-only success points to the separation and DI track. No coverage points to sound-space training.

The larger implication is that arm B and the DI-recovery track are less separate than they looked. Every route to a usable song-only answer, except a one-shot predictor that passes R1, ends at the same bottleneck: something must score candidates against a song without the take's DI. Experiment 4's rebuild is the best-evidenced candidate for that role. The verifier is the only DI-free one with a principled training signal, and the project's history gives it low odds. Sequencing arm B's diagnostics now and the scorer after experiment 4 reports spends the least of the remaining ~56k renders on the question most likely to decide the product.
