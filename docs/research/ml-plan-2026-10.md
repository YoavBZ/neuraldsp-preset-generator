## 0. Amendment 1 (2026-10-14): the goal is now to generate the preset, and a third report

This amendment was made after the plan below was reviewed. **Where it differs from the rest of
the plan, the amendment wins.** The original text is kept so the change can be audited.

### 0.1 What changed

- **The user's goal changed on 2026-10-14** [P, `docs/ROADMAP.md`, PR #187]: "from a song and
  its research, generate the most accurate preset by setting every control the song needs.
  Choosing the closest factory preset is not the purpose; factory presets are starting points."
  - The plan below was written for *choosing* a menu preset. Under the new goal, a menu pick
    is only a **starting point**. The output is a **generated** preset: a start plus searched
    settings, with every stored control written.
  - **Search becomes the main line,** not a late branch. Knob ceiling (A2) now gates the whole
    programme: if searched knobs add little even with the true DI, generation cannot beat a good
    start, and effort goes to choosing the start.
- **Main also gained:**
  - `match` now isolates every Morgan render (PR #186);
  - a short product roadmap, with history moved to `docs/roadmap-history.md` (PR #188);
  - the end-to-end runs of 2026-10-13 (`docs/e2e-runs-2026-10-13.md`).
- **A third research report ("Report C")** answered the updated brief. It is kept as
  [`ml-plan-2026-10-checks/report-c.md`](ml-plan-2026-10-checks/report-c.md); its new claims are
  checked in `C1-report-c-claims.md`.

**Why this order.**

- **Search first:** the new goal is generation, and C′ already exists.
- **The knob-ceiling result comes before any song-side work:** a rebuilt DI cannot be expected
  to beat a true-DI search.
- **A learned score comes only if the rebuilt DI is shown to be the bottleneck.** This is
  Report C's own logic, and it saves ~45–80k renders on the passing path.
- **The carry-over audit and the label table stay early:** B3's evaluation, B1, C1 and F1 all
  read stored or new true-DI scores.

### 0.4 New and changed steps

#### A5. Optimiser bake-off on known, reachable targets (true DI)

- **Question.** Within 150–300 renders, does a covariance-aware optimiser (CMA-ES) or a
  mixed-variable one (TPE) get closer than knob ceiling's coordinate search?
- **Why.**
  - Knob ceiling reads every searched gain as a lower bound because its search falls short: the
    realistic control lands 0.075 short on average, up to 0.26 [P].
  - If (b) reads "little: undecided", only a better optimiser can tell "the menu is near the
    ceiling" apart from "the search is weak".
  - Report C argues covariance adaptation fits the coupled valley. FXRepBench found CMA-ES equal
    to random sampling at equal budget on a different problem [V]. Knob ceiling's simulation
    found random directions no better [P]. So test it.
- **Checked.**
  - knob ceiling's `knob_search` v6 and its realistic-control format exist [P];
  - pycma is BSD-3, Optuna is MIT, and a numpy CMA-ES already exists in `match/search.py` [P/V].
- **Targets.** Knob ceiling's realistic format: the "recording" is a moved preset played with
  the part's own DI, not re-equalised; the search renders the measure DI. The known settings
  give a reachable, non-zero distance.
  - 12 cells: 6 development parts (3 clean, 3 crunch) × 2 amps, one target each, drawn with a
    declared seed.
  - Moves are off the step grid, as in knob ceiling.
- **Arms**, all from the same start, seed and search space as knob ceiling, 300 renders each:
  1. `knob_search` v6;
  2. small-population CMA-ES (λ ≈ 8–12) over the continuous settings, with the mics as an outer
     categorical loop;
  3. TPE (Optuna), with native categoricals.
- **Measure.**
  - Primary: the held-half shortfall, as a log ratio against the target settings' own distance.
  - Secondary: gap closed, 1 − d(found) / d(start) (Report C's measure), and convergence at 50,
    100, 150 and 300 renders.
- **Gate (to declare).** An arm replaces `knob_search` if both:
  - its mean held-half shortfall is at least 0.03 smaller;
  - it wins on ≥ 8 of 12 cells.

  Report C's sanity bar (median gap closure ≥ 70%, ≥ 50% on most targets) is reported. Knob
  ceiling's v6 exact control already closes a median of ~75% by distance [I, from its held/start
  ratios 0.10–0.46].
- **Then.**
  - If an arm replaces `knob_search`, B3's true-DI comparator is re-run with it on B3's cells, so
    the comparison stays like-for-like.
  - If (b) read "little" and the new optimiser finds much more, the menu question reopens.
- **Optional:** a control-sensitivity map (Report C), ≤ 10k renders: local perturbations around
  start presets, to set step sizes and find interacting controls. Run only if A5's winner still
  falls short.
- **Cost.** ~10.8k renders, about 1–1.5 h. No training.
- **Risks.**
  - 12 cells.
  - The only mismatch in these targets is the DI's equalisation, which is much smaller than a
    real record's.

#### A6. C′ for every fold, or a leak-free restriction

- **Question.** Can B3 use rebuilt DIs that no network trained on, on the parts where knob
  ceiling measured K_S?
- **Why.**
  - C′ exists only for fold 2 (`models-cprime/fold2.pt`), so it is leak-free on the 18 pinned parts
    in 8 bands, of which only about 7 are in knob ceiling's 25 [P].
  - Using C′ on parts whose band it trained on would flatter B3.
- **Option 1 (recommended): train C′ for folds 0, 1 and 3.**
  - Use C′'s recipe (`docs/residual-di-plan.md`; `learn/direc.py`), seed 0, each leaving its own
    fold out. About 6 h of MPS each, by R's measured time [P], so ≈ 18 h.
  - The pair caches are rebuilt (~35 GB) and deleted after training [P]. With the 30 GB floor,
    this needs ~65 GB free. **Run it first, before A3's FLACs or any bank fills the disk.**
  - Never run it alongside another MPS job.
  - **Check:** each fold's validation loss is in line with fold 2's.
  - **Check:** on its own held-out fold, its menu pick against the leave-band-out preset falls
    in C′'s known range. Descriptive only.
- **Option 2: restrict B3 to leak-free parts**, counted in code with `train.k3_folds()` at
  declaration.
  - Run the true-DI comparator (K_S) on any of them outside knob ceiling.
  - Cheaper on MPS, but fewer bands (about 8).
- **Cost.** 0 renders either way.

#### B3. Rebuilt-DI search on development (Report C's Plan A): the generation test

- **Question.** Starting from today's class start, does searching the real plugin, scored through
  C′'s rebuilt DI from the song's stem, get closer to the recording than the start? How much of
  the true-DI search's gain does it keep?
- **Why.**
  - Under the new goal the output is a searched preset.
  - C′ is the only song-side signal with held-out evidence:
    - set 4: −0.087 against the old starts;
    - set 5: −0.124 against the old network C;
    - but against today's crunch start only −0.023 [P].
  - Search may amplify the rebuilt DI's known faults (dirty, unrelated above 3 kHz) [P]. Report C
    names the same risk. A development test settles it before any training.
- **Entry.**
  - A2 has reported: if knob ceiling's K_S against S is about zero with the *true* DI, B3 is
    skipped (step 6c).
  - A1 and A3 are done, A5 has chosen the optimiser, and A6 is done.
- **Cells.** Knob ceiling's 25 parts × 3 amps, each starting from today's class start S on that
  amp. Leak-free per A6. Both directions (search on one half, score on the other).
- **Objective.**
  - `rebuilt_distance` (lag −52) between the part's **unfiltered stem** (A3) and F(x̂, p), where
    x̂ is C′'s rebuild of that stem.
  - Diagnostic arm, run only if the stem arm fails: the rebuild from the amp track. It tells
    separation loss from rebuild loss.
- **Search.** A5's optimiser at 150 renders per direction, the same budget as K_S.
- **Scoring.** The selected settings are rendered through the **true DI** and scored by the
  true-DI judge (`flat`, and v3 beside it) on the held half.
- **Comparators on the same cells:**
  - S;
  - K_S (true-DI search from S; knob ceiling, or A5's optimiser);
  - M (the menu oracle);
  - the C′ menu pick through the same rebuilt DI.
- **Known-target control** (Report C), on A5's 12 cells:
  - the "recording" is a moved preset rendered through a hidden development DI;
  - C′ rebuilds the DI from that render, and the search runs on the rebuild;
  - the true-DI arm is A5's result.

  | Outcome | Reading |
  |---|---|
  | Rebuilt arm closes ≥ 50% of the gap on ≥ 8 of 12, or within 0.03 of the true-DI arm's shortfall | Pass |
  | True-DI arm passes, rebuilt arm fails | The rebuilder is the bottleneck: step 6b |
  | Both fail | The optimiser or parameterisation: step 6c |

- **Development gate** (to declare; stem input; held half; band-weighted). All must hold:
  1. B3 against S ≤ −0.03, and ≥ 40% of K_S's gain against S on the same cells;
  2. ≥ 60% of bands better than S;
  3. not worse than the C′ menu pick;
  4. no class (clean, crunch) worse than S by more than 0.03 on its mean.

  Report C's −0.07 is reported beside them.
- **Anti-gaming.**
  - Flag settings at the end of their range (the judge likes spectral extremes [P]).
  - A blind listening audit on 6 cells: the recording, S, and B3's preset through the true DI,
    with hidden repeats.
- **Cost.**
  - ~22.5k renders (25 × 3 × 2 × 150), plus 1.8k for the control and ~150 true-DI scoring renders:
    ≈ 24.3k, about 2–3 h.
  - The amp-track diagnostic arm adds 22.5k.
  - If A5 replaces the optimiser, re-running the true-DI comparator adds 22.5k.
- **Risks.**
  - Crunch has 5 bands in knob ceiling's set.
  - The rebuilt DI's lag and activity fallback (`learn/rebuilt_judge.py`) differ from the true-DI
    judge's, a stated asymmetry.
  - Unfiltered stems of multi-guitar parts may hold the wrong guitar.

#### C3. Proposal model (optional; Report C's Plan C)

- **When.** Only after B3 passes (to cut renders per song) or C1/C2 pass (a song-side score
  exists).
- **What.** An audio encoder with categorical heads (amp, channel, mics, switches, pedal on/off)
  and topology-conditioned continuous heads (a mixture-density head or a conditional flow; the
  FlowSynth idea, reimplemented). It samples 16 complete presets; the plugin renders them; the
  best few seed the search.
- **Gate** (Report C's): the same final quality with ≥ 40% fewer real renders, or ≥ 0.03 better
  at an equal budget.
- **Cost.** Trains on C's bank; ~5k renders for evaluation.

#### Changed triggers

- **B2 (DI ladder):** now runs on step 6b, when the rebuilder is shown to be the bottleneck.
  Otherwise it is optional research.
- **C1 (learned ranker):** now runs on step 6b, or as optional research. Its two heads serve
  generation:
  - H1's ranking picks the **start and topology** for search;
  - H2's embedding distance is a **DI-free score** that B3 can use in place of the rebuilt DI.

  Its development gate is unchanged. A generation readout (B3 with H2 as the score) is added if
  it passes.
- **D2 (song-side search):** becomes **search in `generate`** after B3 passes:
  - 150–500 renders per song, from the class start and the neighbouring start;
  - core knobs re-opened during the cab/EQ stage, not frozen;
  - it outputs two complete presets.
- **D1:** unchanged, plus re-opening core knobs during the EQ stage.
- **E2 (product shell):**
  - writes every stored control: inactive controls get deterministic values;
  - sets the output level by a loudness rule, because the judge removes level; its error is
    reported separately;
  - keeps the two options diverse only within ε of the best (Q_ε).
- **F1:**
  - Automatic product: the primary outcome is the **generated** preset (top-1) against today's
    class-routed start.
  - Hybrid: the best of the two generated presets against the best of the two starts.
  - Secondary: the generated preset against the menu oracle (can generation beat the best menu
    preset?) and Q_ε coverage.

## Summary (before Amendment 1)

*For the current order, read Amendment 1 above. This summary describes the plan as reviewed.*

1. **Do not start a new model yet.** Three things come first, and none of them needs a model:
   - **Render-order carry-over audit (A1).** A newly found render bug may have shifted stored
     scores. The scores behind this plan's own reference numbers are exposed to it.
   - **Knob-ceiling result (A2).** The experiment is already running. It answers "does a perfect

## 3. Ground rules for every step

Each rule is verified in the repo; sources are in A2 item 11.

- **Plan before compute:** plan, then review, then message the product session, then run, then
  audit. Any change is a dated amendment.
- **Every new Morgan render passes `isolate=True`** (knob-ceiling amendment 3).
  - `isolate` fades in over the first 0.25 s, so trim that from any isolated render used as
    audio (B1 prototypes, C1 bank).
  - Tone King already isolates automatically, but reused Tone King processes need a
    repeatability check before any research batch (A2 item 4).
- **Scheduling:**
  - renders share the machine;
  - nothing runs 03:00–04:15;
  - long jobs launch with `setopt no_bg_nice`;
  - the licence daemon is never touched;
  - reuse processes (about 10 daemon ports per new process).
- **MPS:**
  - no `torch.stft` with gradient inside the MPS graph for signals over 65,536 samples;
  - compute features on CPU;
  - never run two MPS trainings at once;
  - clip gradients by hand.
- **Audio:** the measure DI and the renderer run at 48 kHz. Any 44.1 kHz model is wrapped in a
  48 → 44.1 → 48 kHz `resample_poly` (147/160), as E0 did.
- **Disk:** keep 30 GB free, so ~35 GB is usable.
  - Score renders as they arrive.
  - Keep distances, plus short 16-bit FLACs only where the audio is reused.
- **Held-out data:**
  - used only by a declared test, and each use goes in the set's ledger;
  - nobody listens to sets 5 or 6 before a declaration;
  - re-rendering held-out audio, even for an audit, counts as a use.
- **A development-test ledger is created.** The protocol asks for one and none exists.
- **Judge:** the primary judge is `flat` on measfix, cross-half: choose on one half, score on the
  other, both ways. Report judge v3 beside it until the user adopts it.
- **Licences:**
  - weights and tools: open licences only (MIT, Apache, BSD, CC0, CC BY), per the brief;
  - data: non-commercial research licences are already in use (MedleyDB; Mixing Secrets, which
    covers set 5 and the Cambridge sessions). New NC data is the same kind of use.

### C1. Learned ranker pilot (one bank, two heads)

*Amended: runs on step 6b or as optional research. H1 picks the start and topology for search; H2 is a DI-free score for search (§0.4).*

- **Question.** Does a model trained on plugin renders placed in song conditions rank the Morgan
  menu for real development recordings better than today's start and the song-blind medoid?
- **Why, honestly.**
  - The project's three learned routes all trained on solo renders, with few players, no
    separator, and level erased [P].
  - The levers with real evidence are tone diversity (EGDB-PG: 22.8 → 79.0 F1 on unseen tones)
    and pairs with different notes (RelFx ablation: 1.399 → 1.876) [V].
  - Separator-in-the-loop and mixture training have weak evidence (§1.1 #8). No guitar
    render-to-record result exists.
  - So this is a **bet**, run as a pilot with a kill rule.
- **The bank (pilot).**
  - **DIs with a verified role:**
    - the classed development parts (82 parts: clean 48, crunch 17, high-gain 17; a DI is
      valid input whatever its class);
    - Guitar-TECHS P1 (CC BY 4.0);
    - the Five-guitar dataset (CC BY 4.0), once its DI-out is checked to be a pre-EQ DI (G).

    Split into folds by band. The 54 unclassed parts are left out: they include failed
    pairings.
  - **80 DI clips × 111 presets = 8.9k renders.** A third of the clips get a pre-render input
    level of ±4 dB and a pickup EQ; level is the main drive confound.
  - **3k off-menu targets:** a DI clip through random continuous settings in knob ceiling's
    search space (amp knobs, input gain, mics, cab; pedals fixed off). That is about 37 targets
    per clip, each rendered once.
  - **Total:** ~12k renders, about 1.5 h.
- **Labels.**
  - Set-valued: every preset within ε of the best under the judge, with ε set from A4b's half
    noise (around 0.04).
  - A listwise loss on the negative distance carries the full order.
  - **Label scoring is the hidden cost:** a full 111 × 111 matrix per clip plus 3k × 111 for the
    targets is about 1.3M two-half judge evaluations, maybe 1 s each, so ~360 CPU-hours [I].
    - Measure the time per score on the first 1,000.
    - Declare a CPU cap, for example ≤ 48 h wall on 6 cores.
    - Fit under it by labelling in-menu targets only against a top-20 pre-filter (a cheap
      spectral distance) and subsampling targets.
- **Augmentation after rendering** (no renders):
  - **Backings:** mix into band-disjoint real backings (other folds' `backing_instrumental.wav`)
    at −12 to +3 dB guitar-relative. Measure the real guitar-to-backing ratio from the
    development `mix` and `backing` files first; "near 0 dB" is [I]. The old encoder used −12
    to −30 dB bleed [P].
  - **Second guitar:** sum one into some mixes. Multi-guitar stems sit at 0.74 dB SNR, and 54 of the
    61 parts scored there are multi-guitar [P, `docs/separator-upgrade-results.md:14-16`].
  - **Mastering:** bus compression and limiting to −14…−8 LUFS (dasp-pytorch, Apache-2.0, or
    plain numpy), plus codec perturbation.
  - **Separation:** htdemucs_6s on a precomputed half of the mixtures, ~6k separations, about 8
    h CPU [I, from 15 s per 30 s]. Run when the renderer is idle. Training clips are separated
    without the product's 30-s context, a stated mismatch. Expect acoustic and electric guitar
    to mix [P].
- **Inputs.**
  - Two views: the stem and the mix, with their agreement as a feature (Report B).
  - Level kept: no per-clip normalisation, plus LUFS as a side input.
  - Log-mel on CPU.
- **Heads, on the same bank.**
  - **H1, set-valued ranker:** 111 scores; multi-label cross-entropy plus listwise.
  - **H2, prototype encoder** (Report B):
    - contrastive training: same setting / different DI as positives, same DI / different
      setting as hard negatives;
    - ranks by distance to prototypes averaged over other bands' DIs.
  - **Backbone:** a small 1-D CNN like the project's 487k-parameter encoder, from scratch, or
    B1's best frozen row as the initialisation.
  - **Folds:** one model per band fold, 4 folds as in `k3_folds`. Training is a few hours per
    fold on MPS for the small CNN [I]; time it on fold 1 first.
- **Baselines.**
  - Today's class-routed start.
  - The leave-band-out medoid.
  - The shuffled-input control (another part's audio).
  - B1's best frozen row.
  - The records-only ridge from the direct-ranker proposal; needs decision 0.4.
- **Pre-checks.**
  1. **Synthetic-song positive control.** Renders from held-apart bands, planted, mixed, mastered
     and separated. Top-5 must contain an ε-equivalent preset ≥ 60% of the time, and top-1
     regret must be at most half the medoid's. If it fails, the pipeline is broken (not a
     transfer failure): fix and re-run, at most twice.
  2. **Domain probe.**
     - First calibrate it on a real-vs-real control: development stems against other
       development stems from different bands.
     - Then require that augmented render stems are separable from real development stems no
       better than the control + 10 points, with an absolute cap of 75%.
- **Development gate** (to declare; leave-band-out over the A3 parts).
  - **Product input:** the unfiltered stem of every A3 part with a mix. The 25 usable-stem parts
    and amp-track input are reported as diagnostics.
  - **Either head must meet all of:**
    - band-weighted top-1 gain ≥ 40% of the oracle's edge over today's start on the same parts
      (measured in A4a), and ≤ −0.03 in absolute terms;
    - better than the medoid and the shuffled control;
    - Recall@3 (τ = 0.15) above house-3 and random-3 (A4a);
    - ≥ 60% of bands better.
  - **Power:** 15 bands give about 0.57 at −0.05 [I, A3]. The gate is a development screen, not
    a claim.
- **Kill.**
  - The positive control passes but the development gate fails: try one more augmentation recipe
    (no new renders). If that fails too, **close learned DI-free ranking**.
  - The domain probe fails after two rounds: stop.
  - The gain vanishes when the backing pool is swapped for a different one (Report B's stop
    rule): stop.
- **Cost.**
  - ~12k renders; ~8 h CPU separation; label scoring capped by the declared CPU budget; training
    a few hours per fold.
  - Disk: the bank as 6-s, 16-bit FLAC, ~4 GB; separated inputs as float16 log-mels, ~1 GB [I].
