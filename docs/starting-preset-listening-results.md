# Do the starting presets sound closer by ear? Results

Sat 2026-10-13 as declared in [starting-preset-listening-plan.md](starting-preset-listening-plan.md).
Key hash committed before the sittings, the answer sheet
(`starting-preset-listening-answers.txt`) and the scorer committed before the key was
read; the scorer refuses any other key or sheet. Numbers in
`starting-preset-listening-results.json`; code `research/starting_preset_listening.py`.

## Verdict: heard

Through each part's own notes, the listener chose the option `generate` now prefers on
**17 of 21** main trials (81%, one-sided sign test p 0.004), with no "can't tell". C and K
pooled, the condition that X cannot carry it alone: 13 of 16 (p 0.011).

| block | the question | preferred chosen | p (one-sided) |
|---|---|---|---|
| C | clean start against the old template (clean parts, PR12) | 6 of 8 | 0.14 |
| X | clean start against crunch start (clean parts) | 4 of 5 | 0.19 |
| K | crunch start against the old driven start (crunch parts) | 7 of 8 | 0.035 |

Every block leans the preferred way; only the pooled reading decides. X without the two
parts whose song also had a C part: 3 of 3.

## Checks

- **Hidden references:** found in both sittings; neither sitting is void.
- **Reversed taste:** on the one crunch part where the judge prefers the heavier option,
  the listener chose it. One trial is consistent with, but cannot rule out, a general
  preference for lighter gain: in X and K the preferred option is always the lighter
  one, and it was chosen on 11 of those 13 trials. Only block C (6 of 8) and this trial
  separate "closer in tone" from "lighter". For clean and crunch references, lighter is
  plausibly right.
- **Repeats:** 3 of 3 answered the same way with A and B swapped (about 13 trials
  apart in the same session, so memory may help).
- **No position bias:** 10 A and 11 B answers on main trials; always answering B would
  have scored 11 of 21. Both hidden references happened to sit at A (to randomise next
  time).
- **Ear and judge:** on the exact clips played, the judge favoured the product's option
  on 15 of 21 main trials; the ear chose it on 14 of those 15 and on 3 of the 6 where
  the judge did not. Ear and judge agreed on 17 of 21 (a coincidence with the pooled
  17): 3 of 3 where the judge's margin was over 0.15, and 14 of 18 where it was smaller,
  below the range listening had validated before.

## What it changes

`generate` step 4 records, beside each starting preset's evidence, that the listener
preferred it by ear on these development parts, per block and pooled (in-sample, one
listener).

## Limits

The starting presets were chosen by the judge on some of these development parts, so
this shows the ear agrees with the judge where the judge chose, not that the presets
generalise. One listener, one day. Block C covers PR12 only. No single block is decided
on its own.
