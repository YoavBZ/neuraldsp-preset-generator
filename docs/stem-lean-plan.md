# Does the separated guitar lean toward another guitar? (N8, declared 2026-11-05, before it is computed)

**Why.** The deep review's step 4 (`docs/ml-deep-review-2026-11.md`, section 4). From the stem, an amp reader hit the dominant other guitar's amp more often than the target's (0.404 against 0.342, base 0.345; from the clean target 0.487), with no interval, on rows that hold NS sections. N5 and N5b show no stem cost, but on mixes with no other guitars, and F56's gains on the development sets did not reach real songs, where other guitars play, among other causes it could not separate. If the stem follows the wrong guitar, more labels will not fix that. This is the review's own test, run with an interval on a set that holds no NS rows. **It is a diagnostic: zero renders, zero separations, no training beyond linear probes, descriptive.**

**Data.** The tone probe's split ([tone probe](tone-info-probe.md)) on T2's core twins: `mix-t2`, `xlance_g-t2` and part C's targets, summarised afresh on stored mels, since the full twins' 88 extra randomised controls would leave T reading through noise that O's fixed core draws do not have. So T and O come from one preset generator and one fixed S, though T's rows are filtered and O's are not (below). Rows are those whose loudest other guitar, by render level plus gain, is not a double of the target (a double shares the target's preset, so it can show no lean). Its preset comes from T2's render rows, its class from T2's plan. V-real, the NS songs, the panel and the captures are not read. Other guitars here have independent presets, which real bands often do not, so a size here does not carry to songs.

**Two readers per input.** `tone_info_probe.fit_probe`, unchanged, on the same rows. **T** learns the target's labels and **O** the loudest other's, each conditioned on its own amp and class (its amp head on no conditioning, as ever), with the constant fitted to its own training labels. Inputs: (c) the stem, (b) the mix, and (a) the clean target as a control.

**Rows.**
- **Primary, on the stem: O − T,** amp-head hit minus amp-head hit per example, section-clustered t, 90%.
- **T's follow, on the stem,** on rows whose two amps differ: T's answer on the target's amp minus on the other's, section-clustered. This is what a model trained on the target would do.
- **Beside:** each reader's amp hit against its majority share; readable heads of today's 25 plus the amp choice, for T and O; for T's knob answers on heads scored for both guitars, r with the target's label and with the other's by drive, tone and cab (`reads_v3.HEADS`), demeaned within head, T's amp and class, then within section and head, since T's conditioning alone would otherwise read +0.12 to +0.16 toward the target; the difference with a delete-one-section interval. The conditioning alone, beside today's counts. The same rows on (b), so that a lean the mix already has is not charged to the separator.
- **Control:** on (a), T's amp head is readable and O's is not. It takes no conditioning, so it is a fair test where today's counts are not. If it fails, the read is void.

**Reading, first match** (`reading`):
1. The control fails: void.
2. O − T clear above zero on the stem, and the stem's lean clear above the mix's, paired by example: the lead holds. Training or aligning on real stems becomes a proposal for the user, about 19 h for the stems alone, and it does not start without their yes.
3. O − T clear above zero on the stem and on the mix, the paired row not clear: the mix already carries the other guitar more than the target (it is louder on 75% of rows, median +5.0 dB), so this is no lean of the separator's. No step follows.
4. O − T clear below zero on the stem, with T's follow clear above zero: the lead does not hold on this set, and F15 and F17 stand.
5. Otherwise not shown: undecided, and no step follows from it. T's follow is read beside.

**What it can bear.** Two no-audio readers gave a half-width of about 0.06 on the primary row, so a true gap of 0.06 would clear on about half of draws and 0.09 on about 83%. The real half-width came out 0.143, so that was overstated. Not shown is a weak statement here and is not a failure.

**Limits.** A linear probe on summaries; 12 sections from 9 tracks, all of them also in training; self-built mixes, with presets independent by design. The review's figure was from another reader, so a gap between the two is not a contradiction. The core twins read a smaller space than the product's full one. The two draws are not identical: on training rows T's modes are 48% factory, 23% start, 29% wide, and O's 39%, 21%, 40%, the raw draw, since T's rows passed part C's rules, and O's today's knobs spread a little wider (within-cell sd 0.250 against 0.233). The direction of any bias is unknown, so O − T is read beside the same row on rows where neither draw is wide (276 of 623, all 12 sections).

**Outcome (2026-11-05, F61 in the [plan](pipeline-fix-plan.md)).** The control held. O − T on the stem +0.025 [−0.118, +0.169], not shown; stem less mix −0.029 [−0.107, +0.049]. Beside, audited: T's knob answers follow the louder guitar on drive and cab, in the mix as much as on the stem; post hoc, drive reads the target where the target is louder (+0.297 against +0.042) and the other where it is (−0.026 against +0.255). No training on real stems is proposed.

**Cost.** Zero renders and zero separations; CPU, about 2 minutes of summaries, then 8 fits at about 40 s each. Code `learn/stem_lean.py`, on the torch interpreter since `reads_v3` imports torch, `tests/test_stem_lean.py`; ledger `pipeline-fix`, `phase_stem_lean`; output `tone-probe/stem-lean.json`, read once.

*Review (2026-11-05, independent, before any run):* eight required changes, all made. The largest: the first design set the full twins' labels against core draws, which pushed O − T upward with no lean, so both readers now read the core twins. Also the control on the amp head, each reader's own constant, the demeaning, and the mix's row in the reading. A second pass replaced comparing two intervals with a paired stem-against-mix row, added the rows where neither draw is wide, and took the tests through `prepared`, `read_rows` and `_feats`.
