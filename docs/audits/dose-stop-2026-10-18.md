# Audit: the dose probe's stop on It_Was_My_Fault ElecGtr1, pr12 (2026-10-18)

The [dose-probe](../dose-probe-plan.md) run stopped on its declared breakage check:
`dose100 cambridge-It_Was_My_Fault_For_Waiting-ElecGtr1 pr12: S moved 0.0171 from its warm-up on
half A` (tolerance 0.005). This audit finds the cause with 70 diagnostic renders (isolating
renderer, one process at a time, slots checked before each). STOP.json, the cell's record and the
tolerance are left as they were; nothing was relaunched.

## Summary

- **Cause: a render is not clean when the plugin's previous state differs, above all on the first
  render of a process.** On this pr12 cell the first render comes out up to about 24% louder at its
  start, and the excess fades over about 4 s. An amp change leaves a smaller excess. Changing the
  preset or the DI on the same amp sometimes leaves a short one too: it is mostly confined to the
  first second, and it was not characterised fully. The S render that followed was clean. The check
  fired because, in the second launch, the cell's warm-up was the process's first render.
- **This is not caused by the probe's audio.** The anchor (`cprime_eq`) shows the same effect
  (0.0180 on half A). C′'s 1b rebuild shows it too, but smaller (0.0020). The effect is
  deterministic: two fresh processes gave identical scores.
- **Only this cell is affected, but it would stop every remaining probe.** Half A of this part
  (1–5.5 s) is very quiet: the DI's level over 1–3 s is −34.5 dB against its 1–10 s level. The
  next quietest of the 24 parts is −2.1 dB. A decaying excess that is invisible elsewhere
  therefore moves this part's half-A score. Replaying the run's three shards with no comparator
  (each cell's warm-up then S) left the other 23 cells at most 2.8e-5 from their warm-ups.
- **Scores already stored are unaffected.** The completed cells' S checks read 0 to 2.3e-7. In the
  replay, even a warm-up that came first in its process, or followed another amp or preset, moved
  the scored halves by at most 2.8e-5 on every part except this one, because those parts are loud
  from 1 s on. This cell's Σ scores were never stored.
- **Remedy** (to be declared as an amendment before any re-run): before every (cell, probe)
  warm-up, add one priming render at S with that probe's DI. It is counted but not scored. Neither
  the tolerance nor the check changes.

## 1. What was compared

- `renders/dose100/<cell>/pr12.json`: `status: check_failed`, `warm_ups: 2`, `renders: 40`,
  `checks_float` = [[0, repeat_S, A, 0.01709]]. S is Σ's index 0, so the warm-up and S are
  consecutive renders of the same settings and the same DI (dose100). The comparison is 1b's
  matched `decl` score, half A, `LO.deviation` = |log ratio|.
- **First launch** (13:19, before #218's directory fix): the comparator file did not exist yet.
  Its 4 renders (C′ warm-up and S, then the anchor warm-up and S, all on pr12) came before the
  warm-up. The S check passed, all 48 Σ renders ran, and the run failed only on writing the score
  file (the missing directory). The record says `renders: 38` because it is saved every 12 renders.
  The lane really rendered 53 (4 comparator renders, the warm-up and 48), and so did the other two
  lanes. The run's total of 126 renders is therefore an undercount: the real figure is 159. The comparator matched within 3.4e-6. That launch's `S.flac` (13:19)
  is clean.
- **Second launch** (13:47): the comparator file existed, so it was skipped. The renderer was new,
  so **the warm-up was the process's first render**, S was the second, and the check fired after
  2 renders.
- Drag_Me_Down ElecGtr4 ac20 was resumed the same way (warm-up first in its process) and passed at
  2.3e-7. Most first renders are close to clean on the scored halves; this part is the exception
  (below).

## 2. Repeat renders (70)

Cell It_Was_My_Fault ElecGtr1, pr12, at S (22 renders). Scores are `decl` on half A; half B was within 2e-6 in
every render.

| Process | Sequence | Half-A scores | First vs the rest |
|---|---|---|---|
| fresh | dose100 ×3 | 15.5056, 15.2429, 15.2429 | 0.0171 (renders 2 and 3 bit-identical) |
| fresh (repeat) | dose100 ×2 | 15.5056, 15.2429 | 0.0171: deterministic |
| fresh | anchor ×2, dose100 | 14.9051, 14.6395; 15.2429 | anchor 0.0180; dose100 clean after the anchor |
| fresh | C′ ×2 | 14.2855, 14.2569 | 0.0020 |
| fresh | ac20 at its S (anchor DI), dose100 ×2 | 15.3232, 15.2429 | 0.0053: an amp change leaves it too |
| fresh | dose100 at S, S, Σ#47, S, Σ#30, Σ#30, Σ#47, C′, S | S 15.2429 after Σ#47 and after C′; Σ#30 and Σ#47 repeat | on this cell, these settings and DI changes on pr12 moved the scores by ≤ 5e-7; the waveforms differed by up to 3.5e-3 in the first 0.5 s only |

**The waveform** (fresh dose100, first render minus second): the difference is mostly a gain on
the clean render. After fitting a least-squares gain on the clean render, the residual is about 17% of the difference (RMS) from 0.5 s on.
That gain is +0.23 over 0–0.5 s, then +0.14, +0.08, +0.04 and +0.02, roughly halving every 0.5 s,
and it is below 0.005 by 3.5–4 s. From 4.5 s on, the renders agree to about 3e-5. The anchor's
excess has the same shape. C′'s is about 10× smaller from 0.5 s on. Half A starts at 1.0 s, where
the excess is still +8%. There the rendered output is about 18 dB under its 1–10 s level (the DI
is 34.5 dB under), so the excess is enough to move the score by 1.7%.

**Across the 24 cells** (48 renders): three fresh processes replayed the run's shards
(`L._shard(G.r1_cells(), i/3)`), rendering a warm-up and S per cell with no comparator. This is
the situation of every later probe and every resumed cell: each warm-up comes first in its process
or right after another cell's renders. Those were on another amp, or on the same amp with a
different DI and sometimes a different S. Only this cell exceeded the tolerance (half A 0.0171). The
next largest deviations were femme sw50r 2.8e-5 and colour-me-red sw50r 1.4e-5; all others were
≤ 2e-6. In the first second, though, the warm-up waveforms often differ from S by 0.01–0.34
absolute. That includes same-amp cases: Prodigal pr12 after this cell (0.22, still 8.7e-4 after
5 s), Gym_Hours sw50r after Blind_Spots sw50r (same S, 0.14) and colour-me-red sw50r after
Quicksand (0.14). Others are exactly 0: Bloomlight, First_Offering_2 and Long_Way_Home, all pr12
after pr12. So the trigger is wider than a process start or an amp change. The differences stay
harmless only because those parts are loud from 1 s on.

## 3. dose100's audio for this part, against the anchor (zero renders)

From `arms/build.json` and the rebuilt probe (hash-checked):

- dose100's peak is 2.4 dB under the larger of the anchor's and the truth's peaks.
- Its crest factor is 16.6 dB, and the anchor's is 16.6 dB.
- Its LSD to the anchor is 0.056 dB.
- Its peak over the first 0.25 s is +0.5 dB against the anchor's, and over the last 0.25 s −0.01 dB.
- Its 1–3 s level is −34.5 dB against 1–10 s, the same as the anchor's.

No edge spike, no level jump, no onset at the start of the crop. The quiet opening belongs to the
part, not to the probe.

## 4. The other 23 parts (zero renders)

For every part, dose100 matches its anchor:

- **Quiet opening:** the 1–3 s level against 1–10 s is within 0.1 dB of the anchor's on every part.
- **First and last 0.25 s:** peaks within −0.8 to +1.3 dB of the anchor's.
- **Overall peak:** at most +0.8 dB over the larger of the anchor's and the truth's.

Only It_Was_My_Fault ElecGtr1 has a quiet opening. Its 1–3 s level is −34.5 dB; every other part
is between −2.1 and +2.7 dB on the anchor (−2.0 dB on dose100). No other probe's audio shares the property that exposed the effect,
and the property belongs to the part, so the other probes (`shift100`, `full_gl`, `full100`, the
lower doses) have it on this part too.

## 5. Scope

- **This step:** the render loop runs probes in the outer loop and cells in the inner loop, with
  the comparator once per cell (`learn/rebuild_dose.py`, `cmd_render`). So the comparator renders
  absorb the effect only for the first probe on a fresh cell. For `shift100`, `full_gl`, `full100`,
  `dose50` and `dose25`, every cell's warm-up comes first in its lane's process or right after the
  previous cell's renders. This cell is the first of shard 1/3, so each later probe would stop here
  again.
  - The rebuild-gap and rebuild-tone loops run cells in the outer loop: all arms or probes of a
    cell render consecutively on its amp, after the comparator. Even so, their largest deviation,
    7.4e-4, came from this very cell (`arm_rank_s1`, half A, a warm-up that followed another
    arm's renders). So even same-amp warm-ups on this cell are not perfectly clean.
- **Stored scores:**
  - The seven completed dose100 cells are unaffected: their S checks read 0 to 2.3e-7. The
    replay also bounds the worst case on every part but this one: a warm-up that came first in
    its process, or followed another amp or preset, moved the scored halves by at most 2.8e-5.
  - The first launch's renders on this cell were never scored to disk.
  - The comparator values match the stored scores within 3.4e-6, so earlier steps' S scores are
    consistent with this convention.
  - **Residual risk:** inside a quiet-opening cell, a Σ render could in principle carry a little
    of the previous Σ setting. Only two Σ transitions were tested here, and both moved the score
    by ≤ 5e-7. This matters only for this cell's re-run.
- **Elsewhere:** any step that scores the first render on an amp in a process without a warm-up
  would be off on a quiet-opening part. The steps checked here all warm up. This audit did not
  survey the others.

## 6. Recommended remedy (declare before re-running)

1. **An amendment to the dose-probe plan** (before any further render): before every (cell,
   probe) warm-up, render one priming render at S with that probe's DI. It is unconditional,
   because the same-amp cases above show that an amp-change rule would miss some. The priming
   render is counted in the cell's `renders`, is not scored, and is not stored.
   - The check then compares S with a warm-up that follows a render of the same settings and DI,
     which the repeats show to be bit-identical to S (render 2 = render 3).
   - It adds one render per (cell, probe): 144 on R1's 24 cells × 6 probes, about 2% of the
     7,152.
   - The 0.005 tolerance and the check itself stay as declared. By design the check no longer
     sees the first-render effect, which is intended: warm-ups are not scored, and the effect is
     documented here.
   - Optional, before re-running this cell: render a few Σ points twice each, in Σ order, to bound
     the residual risk above. This is cheap (about 10 renders) and must be declared with the
     amendment.
2. **This cell's record** (`check_failed`) and STOP.json: by the plan they are cleared only after
   investigation. This audit is that investigation. Clearing them, and re-rendering the cell's
   dose100 from scratch under the amendment, is for the step's owner once the amendment is merged.
   The first launch's clean `S.flac` can be overwritten.
3. A unit test that a priming render precedes every warm-up and is counted.

**Renders used by this audit:** 70, all at S or Σ points of R1 cells, on the isolating renderer
(warm-up checks: 6 fresh processes, 22 renders; shard replay: 3 processes, 48 renders). None were
stored under `~/ndsp-presets/learn/rebuild-dose/`.
