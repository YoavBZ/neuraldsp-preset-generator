# Audit of the ML negatives, 2026-10-11

Asked by the user: make sure the two trained models really failed, and that we didn't
assume it. Two independent, adversarial auditors re-derived every figure from stored
results with their own code (`tmp/audit-sound-desc.md` and `tmp/audit-di-chooser.md`,
local). No arithmetic, statistics, key or alignment error was found. Several readings
went further than the data.

## The sound translator ([results](sound-desc-results.md))

- **The gates stay inconclusive,** and Phase B correctly doesn't start.
- **"No better than untrained baselines" was a part-weighted reading.** One crunch band,
  Eat The Feeder, holds 15 of 39 cells, and 12 of them are one song (Wickerman, four
  tracks with nearly the same picks). Weighting each band once, on crunch against the
  fixed preset:
  - translator −0.147;
  - rebuilt-DI chooser −0.145;
  - same-notes ceiling −0.163;
  - no learning −0.088.
- **Without Eat The Feeder,** the translator matches the ceiling (−0.164 against −0.161).
- **On Eat The Feeder it picks wet presets** (Spacey Grits, Country Slapback, Wet Room),
  most of the damage on AC20. A hypothesis: the record's room and reverb tails read as
  preset effects.
- **Clean was never a test of it:** even with the same notes the description can't pick
  on clean (ceiling −0.005).
- **The sim-to-real explanation holds for the flexible model, not for ridge.**
  - The MLP fits renders far better than ridge, then collapses on real records.
  - Ridge records do as well as renders in 5 of 6 crunch bands, and distance from the
    renders doesn't predict where it fails.
- **Other simple formulations do no better:** nearest class mean, whitened distance, kNN
  over render labels, tone only, tilt removed.
- **Verdict: undecided, not failed.** With 6 crunch bands and one dominant song, the test
  could not decide.

## The rebuilt-DI chooser

- **The confirmation stands:** −0.087 against the product's starting preset on clean and
  crunch; harmful on high-gain.
- **"Adds nothing over the fixed clean presets" is not established.**
  - That +0.009 used the 3 kHz cut, which hurts on held-out clean parts: +0.044 worse than
    the uncut DI (90% +0.013 to +0.075, 3 better / 16 tied / 20 worse).
  - The earlier "+0.013, without consequence" pooled clean with crunch, where the cut does
    nothing.
  - Uncut, the chooser is −0.035 against the fixed clean presets (−0.085 to +0.016, 6 of
    8 bands better). On development, with the fixed preset chosen leave-band-out, it is
    −0.039 (−0.067 to −0.010).
  - The held-out test had about 20% power for an effect that size.
  - **From a separated stem** (the product's input) development shows no edge (+0.010),
    so not running the chooser on clean songs still stands.
- **Crunch "not shown" is uninformative, not negative.**
  - 3 bands allow 2 degrees of freedom, and power at the observed effect is about 40%.
  - All 3 bands lean the chooser's way (13 better, 5 tied, 3 worse).
- **"Five network attempts failed, the route is closed" is not established.**
  - Each attempt was rightly rejected under its own rule.
  - But every network rebuilds a DI less coherent with the true DI than the recording it
    was given. On held-out clean parts, at 1–3 kHz, the recording is at 0.72 and the
    networks at 0.44–0.50; at 3–8 kHz, 0.41 against about 0.02.
  - So the detail is in the input and the network discards it: a limit of the model and
    loss, not of distortion.
  - **All five shared:**
    - a magnitude-dominated loss;
    - no pass-through path;
    - training renders pushed toward high gain;
    - mostly heavy development parts.
  - Two pre-checks stopped very short runs.
- **The oracle's headroom is an upper bound:** it chooses with the same DI that scores
  it.
- **Minor:**
  - the rebuilt-DI lag is off by about 52 samples (no pick changes);
  - the confirmation's rebuilt-DI renders were deleted;
  - `confirm_run report` broke after set 4 grew (fixed here).

## Decisions taken (user: "go ahead")

1. **Docs and roadmap corrected:** "undecided" instead of "failed", and band-weighted
   results reported beside part-weighted ones from now on.
2. **The 3 kHz cut is off for clean parts:** the uncut rebuilt DI is the declared choice
   for clean in any next test. The cut stays only where it was shown to help.
3. **Two cheap follow-ups, each with a declared plan and review:**
   - a translator description that discounts room and reverb tails;
   - a residual DI network (the equalised input plus a learned correction) trained on
     clean and crunch, whose pre-check is "at least as coherent as its input".
4. **Fresh clean and crunch sessions with DIs** remain needed for any firm answer.

## Later the same day: the MPS gradient bug

- **Every DI network except one CPU pilot was trained on MPS,** where torch.stft's gradient
  is wrong for signals longer than 65,536 samples. Training crops are 144,000.
- **So each learned its spectral-loss term from a corrupted gradient**
  ([residual-di plan](residual-di-plan.md), amendment).
- **Results measured with those networks** stand as measurements: the confirmation, the
  clean and crunch checks, v2, v3 and the 3 kHz cut. But they don't show what a correctly
  trained network does.
- **A retrain with correct gradients** (C′) is running.
